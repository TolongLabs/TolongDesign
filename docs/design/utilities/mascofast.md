## What it is

Mascofast generates a character mascot from a text prompt or reference photo,
then creates variations and poses intended to preserve a consistent character.
The product pages position it for app icons, SaaS brands, and onboarding or
marketing scenes. It also publishes a free mascot gallery.

The generator is a hosted creative service rather than a design component
library. It can provide a starting asset for a product identity, but teams
should still review consistency, brand fit, transparency edges, and animation
behavior before integrating the output.

The terms say users own generated mascots and may use them commercially without
attribution. Gallery assets also have commercial-use permissions but carry
restrictions against reselling or rehosting them as standalone assets.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** A free gallery of mascots is available. Generated output uses paid
  credits or a paid plan.
- **Paid:** Starter is $9/month for 72 credits, Growth is $29/month for 288
  credits, and Studio is $89/month for 864 credits, on monthly billing.
- **Sign-up required?** An account is needed to generate and manage assets;
  gallery browsing is public.
- **Licence for client work:** The terms say users own generated mascots and may
  use them commercially without attribution. Gallery assets have separate
  restrictions, including no standalone resale or rehosting.

## How to use

1. Open the mascot generator and describe the character's role, material,
   palette, silhouette, and expression. Keep the prompt specific to the
   product's existing visual identity.
2. If the workflow accepts a reference photo, use an image you have rights to
   use and avoid uploading confidential client material without approval.
3. Generate variants and poses, then compare them side by side for stable
   proportions and recognizable details.
4. Export transparent assets at the required dimensions. Test the edges on both
   light and dark backgrounds.
5. Read the terms for generated assets and gallery downloads separately before
   handing files to a client.

## When to use it

- Use it when a product needs a character direction or a set of matching mascot
  poses to test in an interface.
- Use generated output as a draft for a brand review, especially when the mascot
  needs to appear across icons, onboarding, and marketing.
- Skip it when the client requires hand-authored vector artwork, a guaranteed
  source file, or fully specified rights before creative exploration.
