## What it is

99designs Discover (alongside its official editorial blog, "The Creative Edge")
is the visual showcase and educational publishing arm of 99designs by Vista.
While 99designs operates primarily as a global marketplace for freelance graphic
designers and design contests, its Discover section and blog serve as public
archives of real-world branding, packaging, typography, and logo design work.

The Discover feed enables users to filter through hundreds of thousands of
completed design projects created by independent designers worldwide. Categories
include logos, brand identity packs, product packaging, illustrations, book
covers, and web UI.

Complementing the visual feed, the editorial blog publishes in-depth annual
creative trend reports (e.g., Graphic Design Trends, Logo Design Trends, Color
Trends), practical guides to color psychology, and breakdowns of historical
graphic movements (such as Art Deco, Brutalism, and Swiss Style) applied to
contemporary brands.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Browsing the Discover showcase feed (`99designs.com/discover`),
  filtering designer portfolios, and reading all articles on "The Creative Edge"
  blog (`99designs.com/blog`) is 100% free without requiring payment or account
  creation.
- **Paid:** Commercial design commissions require payment. Hosting a design
  contest starts at $299 (Bronze tier for logos) up to $1,299 (Platinum tier),
  or direct 1-to-1 freelance hiring with custom project quotes.
- **Sign-up required?** No for reading the blog or browsing project showcases.
  Yes for saving collections, contacting designers, or launching a contest.
- **Licence for client work:** Displayed artwork, logos, and packaging designs
  remain the intellectual property of their creators or the clients who
  purchased them. They serve as aesthetic benchmarks and reference material for
  creative briefs.

Verified on the official 99designs Discover and pricing pages on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Research branding aesthetics: Navigate to `https://99designs.com/discover`
   and filter by industry (e.g., Tech, Finance, Retail) and category (Logo &
   Identity, Packaging) to evaluate how different visual identities communicate
   brand positioning.
2. Formulate client creative briefs: Use real examples from the Discover feed to
   assemble mood boards and establish concrete client expectations for color,
   tone, and visual density before starting frontend development.
3. Review annual trend analyses: Consult the annual Graphic Design Trends
   reports on the blog
   (`https://99designs.com/blog/trends/graphic-design-trends/`) to benchmark
   contemporary visual movements.

## When to use it

- Use it when developing brand identities, brainstorming logo concepts, or
  aligning with clients on aesthetic direction during initial discovery.
- Use it to study diverse packaging and visual branding treatments outside the
  monolithic tech SaaS aesthetic.
- Skip it when looking for frontend code, CSS layouts, or interaction design;
  consult [Codrops](../reading/codrops.md) or
  [Smashing Magazine](../reading/smashing-magazine.md) instead.

## Agent notes

- Non-machine-readable: The platform does not offer an API, CLI tool, or
  `llms.txt` endpoint.
- Discovery URLs are useful as external reference links when generating design
  mood boards or helping users define brand guidelines in specification docs.
- The web interface relies on dynamic client-side hydration for infinite image
  scrolling.
