## What it is

Design Spells collects examples of subtle interaction and visual details from
software products. Entries focus on a small design decision and explain why that
detail is effective or memorable.

The site also publishes newsletter digests and category pages. Its public
material is free and sponsorship-supported; it is a reference source rather than
a component library.

Use it to seed product-specific visual research. A reference should be
translated into a design brief that identifies the behavior, context, and
purpose rather than copied as a surface treatment.

## How to use

- Browse the feed or filter by a visible topic such as mobile or transition to
  narrow the reference set.
- Open a spell or newsletter issue and note the product context, trigger,
  feedback, timing, and visual treatment.
- Save a short reference brief with the user need and behavior to reproduce;
  avoid copying branding or protected assets.
- Prototype the adapted interaction and check keyboard access, reduced motion,
  and small-screen behavior.

## When to use it

- Use it during visual research when the team needs concrete examples of small
  product interactions and details.
- Skip it for tested conversion outcomes or implementation specifications; use
  Good UI for experiment evidence or web.dev for platform guidance.

## Agent notes

- This is inspiration, not a licensed asset catalog or source-code package.
- Capture the reason a detail works and adapt the behavior to the client's
  product context.
- No public API, MCP server, or downloadable data format was found in the pages
  checked.
- The examples are free to view; commercial reuse of screenshots, logos, or
  media is not granted by that access.
