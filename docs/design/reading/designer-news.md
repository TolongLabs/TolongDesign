## What it is

Designer News was a community news and discussion platform for digital design.
Users shared design and technology links and discussed them in a social feed. A
2024 shutdown notice said the service was joining the We Work Remotely family
and would no longer be available; longtime member Sam Solomon also documented
its shutdown in February 2024.

On 2026-10-07, a real Chromium browser received HTTP 402 from
`https://www.designernews.co/`. The page title was “Deployment Paused” and its
body said “This deployment is temporarily paused.” The `/about` and `/pricing`
paths returned the same response. No original discussion archive loaded, and no
Wayback snapshot was found in the archive query checked for this pass.
