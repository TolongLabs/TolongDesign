## What it is

Designer News was a pioneering, community-driven social news board and
discussion platform founded in 2012 by LayerVault. Modelled structurally after
Hacker News, it served as the digital town square for product designers,
typographers, and frontend engineers for over a decade.

Community members submitted links to newly launched digital products, design
system announcements, thoughtful industry essays, typography releases, and job
openings. Submissions were upvoted and debated in threaded comment discussions,
making it a primary launchpad for design tools like Figma, Sketch plugins, and
modern icon sets.

In early 2024, the platform officially discontinued operations as a standalone
discussion forum and was merged into the We Work Remotely brand family. The
original website domain (`designernews.co`) is currently inactive and returns an
HTTP 402 error, meaning the original community discussion archives are no longer
publicly accessible.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** During its active lifespan, Designer News was completely free to
  read, submit links to, and comment on.
- **Paid:** The platform previously offered sponsored job listings for companies
  recruiting design talent.
- **Current status:** The standalone service is defunct and cannot be accessed
  or purchased.
- **Sign-up required?** Inactive. Previously, account registration was required
  to post links and vote.
- **Licence for client work:** Not applicable; service is defunct.

## How to use

1. Note the platform's status: Recognize that `designernews.co` is no longer an
   active source for new links or live design discussions.
2. Replace with modern alternatives: For current real-time design news,
   community discussions, and tool launches, use active modern alternatives like
   [Muzli](../reading/muzli.md), [UX Collective](../reading/ux-collective.md),
   or design engineering channels on X and Bluesky.
3. Consult historical snapshots if needed: If researching historical design tool
   launches from the 2012–2022 era, consult the Wayback Machine (Internet
   Archive) for archived snapshots of Designer News threads.
