## What it is

Degreeless Design is a structured, opinionated roadmap and resource directory
created by product designer Tregg Frank. Born out of the creator's personal
journey of collecting resources throughout design school and early industry
work, the site serves as an alternative to expensive design bootcamps or
university degrees for aspiring product and UX designers.

The curriculum is structured into progressive learning phases:

1. **Basics & Foundations:** Core visual craft—understanding typography (kerning
   games, Google Fonts Knowledge, Ellen Lupton's _Thinking with Type_), vector
   Bézier handles, color theory, Dieter Rams' 10 principles of good design, and
   design history.
2. **Starting UX:** Mastering the end-to-end design process—Laws of UX, Nielsen
   Norman usability heuristics, design sprints, UX research field guides,
   deliverable templates (storyboarding, interaction flows), and design systems
   (Apple HIG, Material Design).
3. **Advanced Resources:** Deepening strategic impact—design leadership, HCI
   ethics, voice/multimodal interfaces, and foundational business books (_The
   Culture Code_, _The Infinite Game_).
4. **Inspiration & Newsletters:** Curated directories of galleries (Minimal
   Gallery, Godly) and leading design newsletters.

The entire resource acts as a high-signal index of the most respected literature
and tools in the digital design profession.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Browsing the entire Degreeless Design curriculum, reading list, and
  external links is 100% free without payment or sign-up.
- **Paid:** None. Degreeless Design is a free public directory. (External books
  linked in the curriculum—such as titles on Amazon or Goodreads—must be
  purchased separately through their respective vendors).
- **Sign-up required?** No for browsing the roadmap. An email address is needed
  only if subscribing to the companion Substack newsletter
  (`degreeless.substack.com`).
- **Licence for client work:** The curation list is free for personal and team
  educational use. Client work follows the individual licenses of the underlying
  tools and design systems linked.

Verified on the official Degreeless Design website on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Establish a design learning syllabus: Use the four progressive sections to
   create a structured onboarding roadmap for junior designers or developers
   transitioning into design engineering.
2. Select high-signal design books: Consult the "Books on UX" and "Books on
   Business" subsections to prioritize physical and digital books for the team's
   reference library.
3. Reference standard UX deliverables: Follow the Deliverables links
   (`https://www.degreeless.design/#resources`) to establish standard templates
   for interaction flows, customer journey maps, and heuristic evaluations.
4. Refresh foundational craft: Revisit the interactive tools linked in the
   Basics section (like The Bézier Game and Kern Type) to sharpen manual vector
   and typographic precision.

## When to use it

- Use it when designing an internal training curriculum or onboarding roadmap
  for developers who want to improve their UI/UX intuition.
- Use it to find vetted book and essay recommendations on information
  architecture, accessibility, and design leadership.
- Skip it when looking for real-time frontend code snippets or CSS libraries;
  consult [Codrops](../reading/codrops.md) or
  [Smashing Magazine](../reading/smashing-magazine.md) instead.

## Agent notes

- Non-machine-readable: The site is a single-page Webflow directory without a
  dedicated API or `llms.txt`.
- Excellent reference for agents when constructing learning guides, book reading
  lists, or explaining standard UX process deliverables to users.
- The external links point to established industry resources (AIGA, NN/g, Google
  Fonts Knowledge, Laws of UX).
