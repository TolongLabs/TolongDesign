## What it is

Frameblox is a comprehensive UI kit, component library, and modular design
system designed exclusively for the Framer website builder. It targets product
designers, agencies, and no-code builders seeking to launch marketing websites
rapidly without reinventing standard layouts.

The kit contains hundreds of pre-styled sections, including hero blocks, feature
grids, testimonial sliders, pricing tables, navigation bars, and footers. Each
section is constructed using Framer's native layout engine, supporting
responsive breakpoints, CMS collections, and built-in interactive states.

In addition to modular section blocks, Frameblox includes complete pre-built
website templates, interactive micro-components (buttons, inputs, notification
banners), and custom Framer plugins intended to accelerate day-to-day visual
workflow.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Frameblox provides free access to a curated selection of smart
  components, Framer workflow plugins, and the Folioblox portfolio template.
- **Paid:** Full access is sold on a one-time payment lifetime access model:
  - **Pro Tier:** $99 one-time payment for freelancers and individual designers,
    granting unlimited websites, personal/client projects, and lifetime updates.
  - **Premium Tier:** $199 one-time payment for teams and agencies, granting
    multi-user permissions and license rights to build client deliverables.
- **Sign-up required?** Yes, via Framer account integration and Gumroad / Lemon
  Squeezy checkout.
- **Licence for client work:** Commercial client projects are permitted under
  both the Pro and Premium paid licenses. Reselling raw template files is
  prohibited.

## How to use

1. Browse the section and component catalog at
   [frameblox.com](https://frameblox.com/).
2. Select a section (e.g. Hero, Pricing, or Feature grid) and click "Copy
   Component".
3. Open your Framer project canvas and paste (`Cmd+V` / `Ctrl+V`) directly into
   the artboard.
4. Adjust Framer color styles, typography tokens, and responsive variant rules
   to match your brand requirements.
5. Connect text layers to your Framer CMS collection if dynamic content binding
   is needed.

## When to use it

- Use it when building no-code marketing sites, agency portfolios, or landing
  pages hosted natively on Framer.
- Use it when non-technical team members or visual designers lead the page
  construction workflow without code compilation.
- Skip it when building code-first React, Next.js, or Astro web applications;
  use [Bearnie](../component-libraries/bearnie.md) or
  [Nexvyn UI](../component-libraries/nexvyn-ui.md) instead.

## Agent notes

- Official website: `https://frameblox.com/`.
