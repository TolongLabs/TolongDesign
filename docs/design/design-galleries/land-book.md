## What it is

Land-book curates live websites and templates for designers, developers, and
marketers. Filters cover industry, style, page type, typography, color,
platform, and other traits; separate areas provide sections, motion, headlines,
OG images, boards, and an Experts Directory.

The free plan is limited by category access, results, and board count. Pro
unlocks unlimited browsing and saves, mobile previews, old versions, and
screenshot downloads.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** Basic browsing is free, with limited filters and results, up to three
  boards, and limited section, motion, headline, and OG-image access.
- **Paid** Pro is $9/month or $6/month on annual billing ($72 billed yearly). It
  unlocks unlimited access, unlimited boards and results, mobile previews, old
  versions, screenshots, and a 15% template discount.
- **Sign-up required?** An account is needed to save boards, submit websites, or
  use Pro. Public browsing is available.
- **Licence for client work** Website entries belong to their creators.
  Templates are separate products with their own platform terms; check those
  terms before client use.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Choose Websites, Sections, Mobile, Motion, Headlines, or OG Images based on
   the deliverable.

2. Filter by industry and type first, then refine with style, color, typography,
   or platform. Save useful references to a board for the project.

3. Open a record and inspect the source site. Use section references to compare
   one pattern across several websites.

4. Turn the board into a short brief with page structure, layout cues, color and
   typography observations, and source links. Keep screenshot downloads within
   the plan and license rules.

## When to use it

- Use it when a team needs deep filters, saved boards, or references across many
  types of websites.
- Skip it for a narrow SaaS-only collection; use
  [Saaspo](../design-galleries/saaspo.md). For minimal sites, use
  [Minimal Gallery](../design-galleries/minimal-gallery.md).

## Agent notes
