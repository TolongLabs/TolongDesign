## What it is

CTA.gallery is a focused reference library for calls to action on websites.
Entries pair screenshots with a short explanation and metadata such as CTA type,
industry and display mode.

The gallery includes button, call-to-buy, download, form, modal, navigation,
newsletter and pricing or subscription patterns. Desktop and mobile captures
help teams compare hierarchy and wording in context.

## How to use

1. Choose a CTA type that matches the job, such as Newsletter, Form or Pricing.
2. Filter or scan by industry and display mode to find examples with a similar
   context.
3. Open an entry and inspect its screenshot, summary and source site.
4. Compare the label, contrast, placement and surrounding reassurance across
   examples.
5. Translate the pattern into a new interface that fits the product’s own voice
   and brand.

**Capture in a TolongLabs design brief:**

- Capture the action and audience state, not just the button color.
- Record label, size, contrast, nearby trust copy and placement in the page
  flow.
- Note mobile differences and define a project-specific success event.

## When to use it

- Use it when reviewing a high-intent action, signup, purchase or download flow.
- Skip it when the task is broad visual discovery; use a full-page gallery such
  as Landing Love.
