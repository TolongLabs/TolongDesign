## What it is

Motionimo is a searchable library of motion-design references, videos, logos,
studios and related resources. Filters include motion type, color and industry,
so it can support visual research for brand, product and campaign work.

The public offer includes a limited free plan, while Pro adds broader viewing
and saving, moodboards, notes, collaborative collections and advanced filters.
The library links to hosted video work rather than offering production-ready
source files.

## Paywall and pricing

- Free: The pricing page lists 9 videos and 3 logos, up to 3 video detail views
  per day and saving up to 5 videos.
- Paid: Pro is $3 monthly, $15 yearly or $29 for lifetime access, as published
  on 2026-10-07.
- Sign-up required? Basic browsing is available; saving and Pro features may
  require an account.
- Licence for client work: The referenced videos belong to their creators; the
  plan does not grant reuse rights.

The access and pricing details above were checked on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Search by a motion concept or filter by type, color or industry.
2. Open a reference and watch the full clip before describing its timing or
   transition.
3. Save a small set of relevant videos or logos within the free-plan limits.
4. Compare motion purpose, easing, duration and relationship to sound or
   interaction.
5. Write original motion specifications for the product, including
   reduced-motion behavior.

**Capture in a TolongLabs design brief:**

- Name the user action or communication goal behind each motion reference.
- Record trigger, duration, easing, direction and the effect on comprehension.
- Link the original video and include accessibility and reduced-motion
  requirements.

## When to use it

- Use it when a project needs examples of animation, transitions, logo motion or
  motion-led storytelling.
- Skip it when you need a code implementation; select an animation library
  separately.

## Agent notes

No public API, CLI, llms.txt or agent interface was documented in the pages
checked. Pro is a browsing and collection tier, not an asset licence.

Agents should link to the creator-hosted video and describe the motion in text.
Do not download clips or treat them as training or production assets.
