## What it is

Behance is an Adobe community for publishing and discovering creative work.
Projects can combine images, video, and other media into a shareable case study;
categories and curated galleries span web, UX, branding, illustration, and more.

Free members can publish without a project-count or media-upload limit. Behance
Pro adds profile and project controls, analytics, boosted projects, zero
platform fees on transactions, and access to Adobe Portfolio.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** Participation, browsing, and publishing projects are free. Adobe’s
  help page says there is no limit on project count or uploaded media.
- **Paid** Behance Pro starts at $9.99/month with an annual commitment; pricing
  varies by geography and commitment. A free trial is offered. Pro includes
  analytics, advanced project settings, and Adobe Portfolio.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is needed to view public projects. An Adobe
  ID is required to publish, follow, save, or use Pro.
- **Licence for client work** Projects belong to their creators. Behance is a
  discovery and showcase service, not a general license to reuse project imagery
  or source files.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Search by discipline, style, or project type, then sort results by
   Recommended, Curated, Most Appreciated, Most Viewed, or Most Recent.

2. Open a project and read the full sequence, not only the cover image. Look for
   the brief, constraints, iterations, responsive states, and final system.

3. Compare several case studies and record the design rationale that applies to
   your project. Save the author and project URL for attribution in the brief.

4. Use the findings to shape a design direction. Do not download or reproduce
   portfolio assets unless the creator provides a license or permission.

## When to use it

- Use it when the team wants process context and full creative case studies
  across disciplines.
- Skip it for fast shot-by-shot visual scanning; use
  [Dribbble](../design-galleries/dribbble.md). For real mobile product flows,
  use [Mobbin](../design-galleries/mobbin.md).
