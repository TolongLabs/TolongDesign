## What it is

Dark Mode Design is a handpicked design gallery curated by product designer Cai
Cardenas. Created to celebrate the craft of dark-themed digital design, the
platform archives websites that exclusively or primarily use dark backgrounds,
deep slate surfaces, and vibrant accent colors.

Designing effective dark-mode interfaces introduces specific challenges:
avoiding pure `#000000` pitch black to prevent harsh eye strain, managing
elevation through subtle surface tints, maintaining WCAG AA/AAA contrast ratios
with muted body text, and ensuring vibrant accent colors do not vibrate against
dark backgrounds. Dark Mode Design curates websites that solve these challenges
with high technical and aesthetic polish.

The showcase focuses heavily on developer tool websites, creative agencies,
crypto and fintech platforms, portfolio sites, and luxury brands—sectors where
dark aesthetics convey technical authority, high craftsmanship, and focused
immersion.

## How to use

1. Analyze background luminance: Inspect live featured sites to see their base
   dark colors (e.g., `#0f172a`, `#121212`, or `#09090b` rather than pure
   black).
2. Study elevation and layering: Observe how cards, navigation bars, and modals
   achieve separation from the background using subtle border strokes
   (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`) and lighter surface tints.
3. Check text hierarchy: Note the contrast ratios between primary headlines
   (bright white/slate-100), secondary body copy (slate-400), and muted labels
   (slate-500).
4. Extract color tokens: Incorporate tested surface and accent tokens into the
   TolongLabs project's Tailwind `dark:` theme configuration.

## When to use it

- Use it when designing developer tools, command-line interfaces, crypto apps,
  or high-tech SaaS products that require an authoritative dark aesthetic.
- Use it to study how to balance glow effects, subtle gradients, and dark
  surface contrast without sacrificing readability.
- Skip it when building daylight-first consumer apps, health portals, or clean
  white editorial sites; consult
  [SiteInspire](../design-galleries/siteinspire.md) or
  [Best Website Gallery](../design-galleries/best-website-gallery.md) instead.

## Agent notes

  alpha white borders).
