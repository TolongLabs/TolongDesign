## What it is

Saaspo curates SaaS websites and page sections. Its indexes group entries by
page type, industry, visual style, assets, and platform, including dedicated
landing, pricing, product, and feature-section collections.

Each listing describes a real site and links outward to the live source. The
site also publishes template listings and free tools; those offerings have
separate prices and terms from the gallery.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** Browse the website and section galleries without paying. The site
  also offers a free weekly email roundup.
- **Paid** The inspiration gallery has no required subscription price shown.
  Templates are separately priced by their makers; examples on the official
  Templates page include free and paid options.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is required for public browsing. Email signup
  is optional; purchasing a template may require an account on its marketplace.
- **Licence for client work** Saaspo listings and screenshots are references,
  not reusable assets. Template licenses come from the template seller and must
  be checked before client use.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Choose Pages, Sections, or OG images based on the design task. Filter by page
   type first, then by industry, style, asset treatment, or platform.

2. Open a page record to see the product description and stack, then follow
   Visit website to inspect the live implementation.

3. For section research, compare like with like: several hero sections or
   several pricing tables. Note copy length, content hierarchy, proof, and CTA
   placement.

4. Create a TolongLabs brief that names the desired structure and audience.
   Treat the Templates area as a separate sourcing decision and verify its price
   and license.

## When to use it

- Use it when the project is a SaaS marketing site and the team needs page-type
  or section-level references.
- Skip it for mobile app flows; use [Mobbin](../design-galleries/mobbin.md). For
  broader styles and industries, use
  [Curated Design](../design-galleries/curated-design.md).

## Agent notes

- No public API, MCP server, CLI, or code package was listed. An agent can use
  public listing pages as reference context, but cannot import a template
  through a published integration.
- Do not treat a listing screenshot as a production asset or assume its platform
  label includes source code.
