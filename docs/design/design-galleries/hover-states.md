## What it is

Hover States (published at `hoverstat.es`) is a curated digital archive
established in 2012 by Mike Guppy and Daniel Powell (originally developed while
working at the London design studio Animade, and now published by Official
Business). The publication is dedicated to exploring alternative, inventive, and
unconventional web design craft.

Unlike standard design galleries that rely on static screen grabs or promotional
hero shots, Hover States documents the living behavior of websites through video
recordings. Every post captures the physical behavior of an interaction in real
time—how elements react to cursor movements, scroll momentum, drag gestures, and
click events.

Each featured entry is accompanied by concise editorial commentary explaining
why the interaction is technically or aesthetically noteworthy, alongside direct
links to the live website and credits to the creators and creative studios
behind the work.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Browsing the entire video archive, reading editorial analyses, and
  following live source links are completely free.
- **Paid:** No paid subscriptions, paywalls, or premium tiers.
- **Sign-up required?** No account creation is required to access any content.
- **Licence for client work:** Displayed websites and interaction recordings are
  the intellectual property of the respective artists, studios, and clients.
  They serve as creative references and design inspiration.

Verified on the official Hover States publication on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Explore the interaction archive: Browse through featured video posts to find
   unusual animation mechanics, scroll-linked typography, or tactile cursor
   reactions.
2. Play the interaction recordings: Watch the recorded clips to analyze timing
   curves, easing functions, and trigger thresholds without needing to
   reverse-engineer the live page immediately.
3. Inspect live implementation: Open the live site URL provided in the entry
   notes and open browser developer tools to inspect CSS transitions, WebGL
   shaders, Canvas contexts, or JavaScript animation libraries (e.g., GSAP,
   Framer Motion).
4. Prototype within design bounds: Extract key motion concepts to build bespoke
   micro-interactions for high-impact brand or marketing touchpoints.

## When to use it

- Use it when designing interactive brand experiences, experimental portfolio
  sites, agency websites, or memorable hero micro-interactions.
- Use it to study how creative developers blend typography, sound, and motion.
- Skip it when building standardized enterprise SaaS applications or
  straightforward e-commerce checkout flows; choose
  [Landingfolio](../design-galleries/landingfolio.md) or
  [SiteInspire](../design-galleries/siteinspire.md) instead.

## Agent notes

- Visual and motion archive only: Hover States has no public API, npm package,
  or `llms.txt` manifest.
- Entries feature embedded video clips demonstrating interface behavior.
- Coding agents can cite Hover States links when proposing creative interaction
  treatments or describing complex animation choreography to human designers.
