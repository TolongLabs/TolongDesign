## What it is

SaaS Landing Page (saaslandingpage.com) is one of the longest-running curated
showcases dedicated specifically to software-as-a-service marketing design. The
directory indexes over 840 production landing pages created by top-tier venture-
funded software businesses and indie SaaS founders alike.

The platform distinguishes itself by organizing full-page captures across
different key pages of a software marketing funnel. Users can explore dedicated
sub-galleries for Pricing Pages, Features Pages, About Us Pages, Blog Indexes,
Testimonial Wall layouts, FAQ sections, and Contact Us forms.

In addition to live landing page screenshots, the platform catalogues high-
performing website templates across popular ecosystems including Next.js,
Tailwind CSS, Framer, and Webflow, providing turnkey design starting points for
new software launches.

## How to use

1. Go to `https://saaslandingpage.com/` and select the page type you need to
   design (e.g. `Pricing`, `Features`, or `About Us`).
2. Examine full-length page screenshots to evaluate how leading companies
   sequence trust markers, product previews, customer logos, and calls to
   action.
3. Observe how pricing tables handle billing frequency toggles, enterprise tier
   contact triggers, and feature check matrices.
4. Draft the page layout hierarchy for your TolongLabs marketing site based on
   established conversion patterns before writing component code.

## When to use it

- Use it when planning the holistic page structure, section ordering, or
  conversion copy flow for a B2B SaaS website.
- Use it to compare how different competitors in the same vertical structure
  their pricing or feature breakdown pages.
- Skip it when you only need isolated micro-interactions or animated components;
  refer to [Sections.wtf](../design-galleries/sections-wtf.md) or
  [Detail Design](../design-galleries/detail-design.md) instead.
