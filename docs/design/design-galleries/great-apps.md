## What it is

Great Apps is a weekly gallery highlighting iOS apps. Each entry links to an app
page and, where available, the App Store, making it a compact way to discover
products and inspect their positioning.

The catalogue is a source of product references rather than a design system. App
screenshots, names and interface details belong to their respective makers and
should be treated as inspiration only.

## Paywall and pricing

- Free: Public browsing and app pages are available without payment.
- Paid: No paid reader plan was published. The apps themselves may be paid.
- Sign-up required? No for browsing the gallery.
- Licence for client work: App artwork and interfaces remain with their makers.

The access and pricing details above were checked on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Browse the current weekly selection or search for an app category.
2. Open an app page to read its short description and follow its App Store link.
3. Inspect the app’s store page and official product site for current details.
4. Compare the way each app describes its audience and primary job.
5. Record design observations without copying screenshots or app branding.

**Capture in a TolongLabs design brief:**

- Capture the app category, audience and core promise.
- Note how the icon, screenshots and store description work together.
- Separate product-positioning ideas from interface patterns that need direct
  study.
