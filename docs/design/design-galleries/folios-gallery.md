## What it is

Folios Gallery is an independently curated showcase highlighting personal and
studio portfolio websites that push beyond standardized minimalist templates.
Maintained by designer Ali (aaali.xyz), the archive curates digital identities
distinguished by unexpected typography, playful interaction models, and bespoke
visual craft.

The collection is cleanly structured into two primary segments: Individual
designer portfolios and Creative Studio portfolios. Rather than indexing generic
resume sites, the curator selects portfolios that function as creative
statements in their own right, combining interactive case studies, dynamic color
systems, and tailored micro-interactions.

Each entry presents a high-definition video walkthrough and direct links to the
creator's live site, making it easy to examine responsive behaviors and
performance across desktop and mobile browsers.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** All indexed portfolios, individual profiles, and video recordings
  are freely accessible with no paywall or mandatory login.
- **Paid:** No paid subscription tier exists. Creator support is funded via
  optional community tips (Buy Me a Coffee) and sponsor placements.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is required to browse. Submissions are
  handled via a free external Tally form (`submit.folios.gallery`).
- **Licence for client work:** Showcased portfolios are the copyright and
  intellectual property of the respective designers and studios. Use them as
  creative inspiration and architectural benchmarks, never as cloned assets.

Verified on the official Folios Gallery site on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Open `https://folios.gallery/` and toggle between the `Individual` and
   `Studio` tabs depending on whether you are designing a solo portfolio or an
   agency presence.
2. Review the preview videos to observe how top creators structure personal
   narratives, project case studies, and interactive contact forms.
3. Click through to live sites to inspect frontend techniques—such as custom
   scroll handlers, SVG masking, and fluid type scales.
4. Synthesize structural takeaways into TolongLabs personal branding or client
   portfolio briefs.

## When to use it

- Use it when designing personal websites, digital agency hubs, or creative
  director portfolios that need a memorable visual voice.
- Use it to study alternatives to cookie-cutter resume layouts and standard grid
  templates.
- Skip it when seeking enterprise UX case studies or B2B SaaS marketing
  patterns; use [Wall of Portfolios](../design-galleries/wall-of-portfolios.md)
  or [SaaS Landing Page](../design-galleries/saas-landing-page.md) instead.

## Agent notes
