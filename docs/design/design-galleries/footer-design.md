## What it is

footer.design, also branded Footr Design in its records, collects website
footers and lets readers filter by styles such as typographic, illustrative,
grid, animated, bright, dark, large type, and bold.

Each entry points to a real website, so developers can inspect how the footer
connects navigation, legal links, newsletter prompts, social links, and the
page’s visual ending.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** The public gallery, style filters, and individual records are
  available without a paid account.
- **Paid** No paid browsing tier or asset price was published on the official
  pages checked.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is required to browse. A submission link is
  available to contributors.
- **Licence for client work** The gallery does not publish rights to reuse
  screenshots or source-site content. Treat each footer as a reference and build
  an original implementation.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Choose a style filter such as Large Type, Grid, Animated, or Small Type to
   match the problem you are solving.

2. Open an entry and inspect its live source page. Note the footer’s information
   architecture, number of link groups, hierarchy, and relationship to the final
   CTA.

3. Compare examples from different industries so you can separate the pattern
   from the source brand’s content.

4. Write the required link groups and behaviors for the TolongLabs site, then
   implement them with accessible headings, focus states, and responsive layout.

## When to use it

- Use it when the page needs a deliberate site ending, useful link grouping, or
  a footer CTA.
- Skip it for full-page references; use
  [Curated Design](../design-galleries/curated-design.md). For navigation
  patterns, use [navbar.design](../design-galleries/navbar-design.md).
