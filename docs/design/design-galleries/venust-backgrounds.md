## What it is

VenustAI publishes AI-generated backgrounds in themed collections and gives each
image an original prompt. The library includes formats for wide desktop heroes,
landscape pages, mobile layouts, and cards, plus a small hero mockup builder.

The site states that the downloadable images, website-example images, and saved
prompts are dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal. The dedication does not cover the
website code, third-party fonts, or trademark rights.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** The full background library and prompts are free to browse and
  download. No account or payment is required.
- **Paid** No library price or paid download tier was published. Custom imagery,
  websites, and software are separate agency services with separate terms.
- **Sign-up required?** No sign-up is required for downloads or the Hero
  Builder.
- **Licence for client work** The official license page dedicates library images
  and saved prompts under CC0 1.0 Universal, for personal, client, and
  commercial work without attribution. It excludes site code, third-party fonts,
  logos, and other rights owned by others.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Open Themes and search by subject, mood, or style. Filter by a broad category
   such as Nature, Landscape, Abstract, or Editorial.

2. Open a theme and choose a format suited to the layout: widescreen for a hero,
   landscape for content, portrait for mobile, or square for cards.

3. Use the original prompt as a starting point when art direction needs a
   variation. Copy the prompt and adapt it in the image generator used by the
   project.

4. Download the image directly and link to the source in project notes. Keep
   brand marks and third-party fonts separate because they are outside the CC0
   dedication.

- [Celestial hours](https://backgrounds.venust.ai/themes/celestial-hours) — A
  sky collection organized by screen orientation and intended use.

## When to use it

- Use it when a client needs free, prompt-paired background art with a clear
  commercial license.
- Skip it when the brief needs a custom brand identity or human-authored
  photography; use
  [Backgrounds Supply](../design-galleries/backgrounds-supply.md) for a paid
  curated asset collection.
