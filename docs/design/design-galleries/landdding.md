## What it is

Landdding is a catalogue of creative landing pages with daily and weekly views,
category and tag filters, color cues, designer profiles and collections. Entries
include a short description and link to the source website.

The site has a free browsing tier and a Pro plan that removes ads and expands
access to search, filters, templates, profiles and collections. It is a
reference catalogue, not a licence to copy submitted page designs.

## Paywall and pricing

- Free: The free plan includes the public feed and limited browsing, search and
  filtering.
- Paid: Pro is $4 monthly or $48 yearly, as published on the official page.
- Sign-up required? Browsing is available without payment; account features and
  Pro access may require sign-in.
- Licence for client work: Referenced website designs remain with their
  creators; linked templates have their own licences.

The access and pricing details above were checked on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Browse Daily or Weekly and choose a relevant category such as SaaS, agency or
   portfolio.
2. Filter by tags, color or designer when narrowing a reference set.
3. Open an entry to inspect its summary, palette, tags and source website.
4. Follow the source link and compare the live experience at desktop and mobile
   sizes.
5. Record structural patterns and create a distinct page brief for the project.

**Capture in a TolongLabs design brief:**

- Write the audience, product promise and intended conversion before choosing a
  page reference.
- Record section order, palette, type and proof points as observations.
- Check whether any linked template has a separate commercial licence before
  purchase or use.

## When to use it

- Use it when you want a fresh stream of landing pages with metadata and color
  cues.
- Skip it when you need motion recordings; use Landing Love for scrolling video
  captures.
