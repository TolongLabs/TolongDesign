## What it is

Landing Love is a gallery of full-page website captures presented as video,
preserving scrolling and motion that a static screenshot would miss. Visitors
can browse categories and filter by visual style, platform, year or studio.

The site’s archive includes product, portfolio and other page types. Public
captures are useful for studying pacing and page structure; the site notes that
the displayed websites belong to their respective owners.

## How to use

1. Browse the site archive or open a category such as Minimal.
2. Use style or platform filters to focus the set on the kind of page being
   designed.
3. Play a capture and note where motion, transitions and sticky elements occur
   during scroll.
4. Compare the page sequence and information hierarchy across several
   references.
5. Translate observations into a project-specific layout and motion brief.

**Capture in a TolongLabs design brief:**

- Record the page goal, audience and the sequence of sections.
- Describe motion by trigger, duration and user purpose rather than copying its
  appearance.
- Note which patterns must be adapted for accessibility, reduced motion and
  mobile.

## When to use it

- Use it when evaluating the rhythm and interaction of a complete marketing or
  portfolio site.
- Skip it when you only need CTA-level detail; use CTA.gallery for focused
  action patterns.

## Agent notes

was documented in the pages checked.

Do not download or reuse captured site content. Link to the original site and
record descriptive observations instead.
