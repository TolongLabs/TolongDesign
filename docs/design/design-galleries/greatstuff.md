## What it is

GreatStuff is a curated archive for creative people, with categories including
AI, tools, resources, assets, development, curations, insights and lighter
material. Visitors can browse the archive, shuffle recommendations and subscribe
to a weekly newsletter.

Entries link to external products, articles or resources. The site is a
discovery layer; verify the provider’s current functionality, security, price
and licence before using a listing in client work.

## Paywall and pricing

- Free: The public archive can be browsed without payment.
- Paid: No reader subscription was published; sponsorship or submission
  arrangements are separate.
- Sign-up required? No for browsing; newsletter signup is optional.
- Licence for client work: Each linked destination controls its own licence and
  terms.

The access and pricing details above were checked on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Browse a category or use the shuffle control to discover a resource.
2. Open an entry and read its summary before following the destination link.
3. Check the provider’s official terms and current pricing.
4. Compare the resource with the project’s actual workflow and technology
   constraints.
5. Keep the source URL and any licence notes in the project decision record.

**Capture in a TolongLabs design brief:**

- State what problem the resource addresses and who will use it.
- Record the destination’s licence, pricing and technical requirements.
- Treat editorial selection as a discovery signal, not a product endorsement.

## When to use it

- Use it when you want a curated, varied stream of tools and creative
  references.
- Skip it when you need a deep technical comparison; verify candidates at their
  official sites.
