## What it is

loadmo.re is an independent, hand-curated digital archive created by French
designer, educator, and creative technologist Kim Boutin (Kim Lê Boutin).
Established from research begun in 2017 and publicly launched in 2021, the
project was created to address a critical void: mainstream design galleries
almost exclusively prioritize desktop viewports, treating mobile layouts as
afterthoughts.

The platform is founded on the conviction that mobile-first web design deserves
its own critical space. Rather than archiving standard responsive business
sites, loadmo.re curates websites that genuinely explore the tactile potential
of the mobile device: thumb-driven swipe carousels, gyroscope and sensor
integrations, kinetic typography scaled for vertical screens, experimental audio
controls, and playful brutalist navigation.

Every listing is presented within an authentic mobile viewport frame, capturing
how the experience feels in the hand. The platform operates completely
independently—with no automated algorithms, no sponsored listings, and no
submission fees.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Completely free to browse, search, and explore. All archives, tag
  filters, and live external links are accessible without payment.
- **Paid:** No paid subscriptions, paywalled posts, or fees. The archive is an
  independent creative initiative maintained by Kim Boutin.
- **Sign-up required?** No. All content is publicly accessible without user
  registration.
- **Licence for client work:** Featured mobile websites remain the intellectual
  property of their respective creators and studios. They are documented for
  critical study, design research, and creative inspiration.

## How to use

1. Filter by interaction or aesthetic tag: Browse specialized tags such as
   `tag:3d`, `tag:glitches`, `tag:editorial`, `tag:brutalist`, or
   `tag:digital-exhibition`.
2. Test on a physical mobile device: Open the live site URLs on an actual mobile
   phone to test touch responsiveness, haptic feedback triggers, and scroll
   inertia.
3. Inspect mobile viewport adaptations: Study how innovative sites handle the
   address bar collapse (`dvh` units), safe-area insets, and thumb-zone
   reachability.
4. Translate into creative briefs: Incorporate documented mobile interaction
   patterns into TolongLabs experimental web builds and client brand showcases.

## When to use it

- Use it when designing mobile-first brand campaigns, digital exhibitions,
  creative studio portfolios, or interactive mobile storytelling sites.
- Use it to study tactile mobile ergonomics, unconventional navigation pills,
  and vertical typography.
- Skip it when building conventional desktop enterprise software or standard B2B
  SaaS landing pages; consult
  [Landingfolio](../design-galleries/landingfolio.md) or
  [SiteInspire](../design-galleries/siteinspire.md) instead.
