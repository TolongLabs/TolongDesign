## What it is

Wall of Portfolios is a specialized web archive dedicated to indexing
outstanding personal websites, interactive case studies, and career portfolios
from product designers, UI/UX professionals, and design engineers worldwide.
Founded and curated by Praneeth J (@Praneethj54), the directory showcases talent
working at tier-one product companies including Microsoft, Amazon, Uber,
PhonePe, and CRED.

Each portfolio featured in the index undergoes qualitative editorial review. The
site publishes monthly editorial breakdowns ("Portfolio of the Month") that
analyze the creator's systems thinking, storytelling narrative, visual identity,
and interactive craft.

The archive offers granular filtering options that go well beyond aesthetic
tags. Users can segment portfolios by professional role (Product Designer,
Design Engineer, Motion Designer, Visual Designer), domain skill (SaaS, B2B,
Dashboard, Illustration), visual aesthetic (Minimal, Dark Theme, Interactive,
Modern Layouts), and past corporate affiliation.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Accessing the entire directory, reading detailed case study
  breakdowns, filtering portfolios, and using the job tracker resources is
  completely free.
- **Paid:** No paid subscriptions or gated archives.
- **Sign-up required?** Browsing is open without an account. Submitting a
  portfolio or saving bookmarks requires free Google OAuth sign-in.
- **Licence for client work:** Portfolios, resumes, and project case studies are
  the intellectual property of the respective designers. Content is intended as
  architectural inspiration and professional benchmarking.

Verified on the official Wall of Portfolios site on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Navigate to `https://www.wallofportfolios.in/` and choose your filtering
   criteria—such as `Interactive Design Portfolios`, `SaaS`, or `Dashboard`.
2. Review the editorial summaries on selected profiles to see why each
   portfolio's narrative and system thinking succeeded.
3. Click through to the creator's live domain to examine layout structure, case
   study depth, prototype embeds, and mobile responsiveness.
4. Apply proven portfolio structures—such as problem-to-metric storytelling,
   interactive prototype embeds, and polished about pages—to your own team or
   client resumes.

## When to use it

- Use it when designing a personal design engineering or product portfolio,
  structuring UX case studies, or presenting systems-level design work.
- Use it to observe how top tech designers balance aesthetic craft with metrics-
  driven business outcomes.
- Skip it when looking for wild, experimental web art or avant-garde creative
  coding; consult [wwwtf.site](../design-galleries/wwwtf-site.md) or
  [Folios Gallery](../design-galleries/folios-gallery.md) instead.
