## What it is

Collect UI organizes interface concepts into numbered challenges such as Sign
Up, Checkout, Landing Page, User Profile, Pricing, Inbox, and Mobile Menu. Each
challenge groups submissions and links to the work’s author.

The site describes its curation as based on Dribbble shots and updated daily. It
also promotes a Chrome extension for uploading and browsing similar designs; the
challenge gallery itself is public.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** The challenge archive and public design pages are free to browse.
  Email subscription for daily inspiration is optional.
- **Paid** No paid membership or browsing plan was published on the official
  pages checked. Sponsor listings are separate from gallery access.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is needed to browse. Email is required only
  for the optional newsletter; the extension may require a browser install.
- **Licence for client work** Design examples are credited to their creators and
  generally link to third-party work. The gallery does not grant a license to
  reuse the designs or images.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Open Challenges and choose the exact UI task, such as User Profile, Inbox,
   Landing Page, or Progress Bar.

2. Use the category feed to compare multiple treatments of the same task. Sort
   where available and inspect author credits.

3. Open linked work for a larger view and note hierarchy, empty states, data
   density, and interaction cues that fit the project.

4. Convert the observations into requirements for the new UI. Use the challenge
   as a pattern prompt, not as a source of production-ready assets.

## When to use it

- Use it when the UI task is specific and you want to compare many visual
  treatments quickly.
- Skip it for live marketing websites or end-to-end product flows; use
  [Curated Design](../design-galleries/curated-design.md) or
  [Mobbin](../design-galleries/mobbin.md).
