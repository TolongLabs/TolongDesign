## What it is

SaaSFrame is a specialized design resource library built specifically for
creators, product managers, and growth engineers working in the SaaS ecosystem.
The platform indexes over 5,000 real-world design examples from leading B2B and
consumer software companies.

Unlike broad landing page galleries that focus only on top-of-funnel marketing
homepages, SaaSFrame covers the entire customer lifecycle. It catalogues three
distinct content layers: marketing pages (pricing pages, feature pages,
comparisons, and product-led growth hubs), product flows (signup, onboarding,
workspace setup, and billing management), and lifecycle email sequences.

The library features search-by-text inside screenshots, side-by-side desktop and
mobile comparisons, and downloadable Figma assets for Pro subscribers, making it
a comprehensive reference for SaaS conversion optimization.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Browsing basic gallery listings, viewing a subset of featured
  landing pages, and previewing flow summaries without a subscription.
- **Paid:** Pro membership unlocks the full library of 5,000+ screenshots,
  complete product flows, onboarding email teardowns, and Figma source files.
  Pro is priced at $14/month on monthly billing or $139/year on annual billing.
  Payments are processed via Lemon Squeezy with a 7-day refund guarantee.
- **Sign-up required?** Browsing public previews requires no login; accessing
  full screens, full flows, and Figma downloads requires an active Pro
  subscription.
- **Licence for client work:** Displayed screens and emails depict third-party
  software products for research and benchmarking purposes. They cannot be
  copied wholesale for production applications.

Verified on the official SaaSFrame website on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Filter by page type or funnel stage: Navigate to marketing pages (e.g.,
   Pricing, vs Competitor, Social Proof) or select email sequences (e.g.,
   Welcome, Trial Ending, Re-engagement).
2. Analyze conversion mechanics: Review how top SaaS brands structure feature
   comparison matrices, articulate value propositions, and handle checkout
   objections.
3. Benchmark lifecycle communication: Examine the pacing, copy, and layout of
   automated onboarding email series sent by fast-growing startups.
4. Export references: Download Figma files or capture layout references to
   assemble marketing wireframes and growth experiments for TolongLabs projects.

## When to use it

- Use it when designing end-to-end SaaS experiences: marketing landing page,
  product onboarding, and lifecycle email onboarding sequences.
- Use it to benchmark pricing page structures, feature matrices, and conversion
  copywriting.
- Skip it when seeking experimental art direction or avant-garde portfolio
  inspiration; use [Awwwards](../design-galleries/awwwards.md) or
  [SiteInspire](../design-galleries/siteinspire.md) instead.
