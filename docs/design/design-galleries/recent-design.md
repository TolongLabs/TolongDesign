## What it is

Recent is a curated feed for websites and broader digital design, including
interfaces, branding, motion, OG images, and App Store screenshots. Its info
page says it grew from an X-based design feed and expanded in 2026 to additional
sources and formats.

The site pairs cards with categories and sometimes a short description, source
URL, and interaction tags. Separate website, OG-image, screenshot, and skills
areas help narrow the research task.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** The public design and website feeds are accessible without a paid
  account. The site also has optional sponsorship and job listings.
- **Paid** No paid browsing tier was published. Sponsorship and job posting are
  separate services; pricing is not shown on the inspiration pages checked.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is needed to browse public pages. Account
  features may be required for saving or submitting.
- **Licence for client work** Recent links to independent projects and does not
  grant reuse rights for their images or design. Follow the source creator’s
  license.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Choose Websites for live-site inspiration, or use Design for a broader mix of
   web, interface, branding, motion, and illustration work.

2. Filter the feed by product category, then open an entry to read its summary,
   source, style, and interaction tags.

3. Use the separate OG Images or App Store Screenshots pages when the
   deliverable is social sharing or app-store presentation rather than a
   website.

4. Build a brief from a few links: record the audience, content structure,
   motion intensity, and visual traits to adapt. Keep original assets linked,
   not copied.

  Open Graph images, including products such as Notion and Mobbin.

## When to use it

- Use it when the team wants a current, wider view of digital product and web
  work.
- Skip it when you need more complete live-site metadata or page-section
  samples; use [Curated Design](../design-galleries/curated-design.md) or
  [Land-book](../design-galleries/land-book.md).

## Agent notes
