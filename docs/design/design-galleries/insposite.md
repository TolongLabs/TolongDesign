## What it is

Insposite is a directory for people who build, combining portfolio links, visual
inspiration and tools. The home page separates Portfolio, Inspiration and Tool
types and offers a submission route for new sites.

The page links to a public GitHub repository for the site implementation; its
README identifies an MIT licence. That licence applies to the repository code,
not to the third-party portfolio and inspiration content listed by the
directory.

## Paywall and pricing

- Free: Public browsing and category filters are available without payment.
- Paid: No reader subscription was published.
- Sign-up required? No for browsing the directory.
- Licence for client work: The site’s linked references belong to their
  creators; the separate Insposite source repository is MIT-licensed.

The access and pricing details above were checked on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Choose Portfolio, Inspiration or Tool from the directory filters.
2. Open a card and follow its original website link.
3. Compare portfolios by role, interaction quality and information hierarchy.
4. For tools, verify official documentation and licences at the provider.
5. Capture a short design brief from observed patterns rather than reusing the
   listed work.

**Capture in a TolongLabs design brief:**

- Record which portfolio or reference informed a specific design decision.
- Describe the pattern and why it fits the product audience.
- Keep third-party media out of project assets unless the rights holder grants
  permission.

## When to use it

- Use it when reviewing portfolios, finding visual references or exploring
  design tools in one place.
- Skip it when you need deep brand-system metadata; use Rebrand Gallery for
  identity applications.
