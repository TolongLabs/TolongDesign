## What it is

Minimal Gallery is a curated collection of live websites, selected for visual
restraint and functional craft. Entries are grouped by subject tags such as
portfolio, agency, SaaS, and e-commerce, and link to the original websites.

The site also has Templates and Tools directories. Some linked template products
are paid; the gallery itself is available without a paid subscription. The
collection has run since 2013 and the site reports an acquisition in 2026.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** Browse website entries and category pages for free. The weekly email
  digest is optional.
- **Paid** No gallery subscription price was published. Templates and tools
  listed on the site may be sold by their creators at separate prices.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is required to browse. Newsletter signup is
  optional; submissions use a form.
- **Licence for client work** The site terms allow personal viewing and
  bookmarking, but prohibit commercial or public reuse of its screenshots and
  thumbnails. Follow the source site’s terms for its own assets.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Start from a subject tag such as SaaS, Agency, or Portfolio, or search the
   gallery for a product or visual trait.

2. Open an entry to read its category and credits, then follow the link to the
   live website. Review the page at desktop and mobile widths.

3. Collect three or four references and note the common traits: amount of
   whitespace, type scale, image treatment, navigation, and page length.

4. Turn those observations into a brief with explicit constraints for the
   TolongLabs site. Use a linked template only after checking its price,
   platform, and license.

## When to use it

- Use it when the brief calls for minimal, clear, or functional web design
  references.
- Skip it when you need advanced filters or section-by-section comparisons; use
  [Curated Design](../design-galleries/curated-design.md) or
  [Land-book](../design-galleries/land-book.md).

## Agent notes

- There is no public API, MCP server, CLI, or package for the gallery. Provide
  the agent with entry links and a short list of traits to compare.
- The site terms explicitly restrict reusing its gallery screenshots and
  thumbnails. Link to originals instead of downloading them.
