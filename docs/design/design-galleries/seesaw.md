## What it is

SEESAW publishes a handpicked feed of websites and product pages, with
categories such as Agency, AI, Design Tools, Developer Tools, E-commerce, and
Portfolios. The homepage says entries are updated daily.

Individual records include a title and one-line product description, then link
to the live site. The Subscribe control collects readers for updates; public
browsing remains open.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** The gallery and its public website records are free to browse. A free
  email subscription is optional.
- **Paid** No browsing subscription or paid access plan was published on the
  official pages checked. Sponsorship is offered separately; its price is not
  published there.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is needed to browse. Email is needed only for
  the optional subscription.
- **Licence for client work** SEESAW links to third-party sites and does not
  grant rights to their design or assets. Use the pages as visual references and
  review the source site directly.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Choose a category from the top row, such as AI, Agency, or Developer Tools,
   to focus the feed on a product type.

2. Open a record to read its short description and follow the source link.
   Compare the working page, not only the gallery title or card.

3. Save links and note what works: product positioning, hero hierarchy,
   navigation, proof, and page rhythm. Separate a visual observation from the
   product copy.

4. Convert the notes into a brief for the TolongLabs project, with the target
   user, required content, and a few permitted visual principles.

  public-service site example in the changing daily feed.

## When to use it

- Use it when the team wants a quick, current scan of product websites with
  concise category labels.
- Skip it when you need many filters or inspectable page sections; use
  [Curated Design](../design-galleries/curated-design.md) or
  [Saaspo](../design-galleries/saaspo.md).

## Agent notes

- No public API, MCP server, CLI, or code package was listed. Link an agent to
  specific website records and ask it to summarize visible structure and copy
  hierarchy.
- The feed changes frequently; record the source URL and date when using a
  reference in a design brief.
