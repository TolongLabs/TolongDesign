## What it is

Dribbble is a community where designers publish visual “Shots,” organize work
into profiles, and offer services. Tags and category pages make it useful for
quick exploration of web, product, illustration, and brand concepts.

The platform is oriented around authored concepts and presentation, not
necessarily production-ready interfaces. Pro membership adds discovery and
client-lead features for designers; it is not needed to browse public work.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** Public shots and profiles are free to browse. A free account supports
  publishing and some portfolio features.
- **Paid** Pro Lite starts at $4/month billed yearly; Standard is listed at
  $4/month billed yearly on the current offer, and Plus at $49/month billed
  yearly. Plans add ranking, leads, analytics, services, and team features.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is needed for public browsing. Sign-in is
  needed to save, publish, or use member features.
- **Licence for client work** Shots are owned by their creators. Dribbble does
  not grant a general license to reuse a design or its imagery in client work.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Search by a project need such as “website landing page,” then use tags,
   color, or the New & Noteworthy feed to find relevant concepts.

2. Open a Shot and inspect its creator, palette, and related work. Check whether
   the image depicts a concept or an actual shipped page.

3. Save a small set of links and note each reference’s useful quality:
   composition, type treatment, color, or illustration.

4. Turn those qualities into a brief. Keep the client’s content and interaction
   requirements as the implementation source of truth.

## When to use it

- Use it when the project needs visual exploration, portfolio references, or
  designer discovery.
- Skip it when you need verified live-site behavior or full case studies; use
  [Behance](../design-galleries/behance.md) or
  [Mobbin](../design-galleries/mobbin.md).

## Agent notes

- No public MCP server or coding package was listed for the gallery. The public
  search pages are useful as human-provided reference URLs.
- Treat a Shot as inspiration, not an approved asset or production
  specification. Check current Pro benefits before recommending membership.
