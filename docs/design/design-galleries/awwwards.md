## What it is

Awwwards is an established digital design competition platform and web
inspiration archive. Founded to recognize the talent and effort of web
designers, developers, and agencies worldwide, it indexes and evaluates live web
projects across aesthetic craft, usability, and technical execution.

The platform hosts thousands of indexed entries submitted by independent
creatives and digital studios. Submissions undergo a structured evaluation
process where an international jury and the community rate websites on Design
(40%), Usability (30%), Creativity (20%), and Content (10%). Top projects
receive accolades such as Site of the Day (SOTD), Developer Award, Site of the
Month (SOTM), and Site of the Year.

Unlike utility galleries focused strictly on standard SaaS landing pages or
isolated UI components, Awwwards showcases high-production, boundary-pushing web
craft. It is a key reference for benchmarking complex animations, interactive
storytelling, 3D graphics, audio integration, and custom typography treatments.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Browsing all galleries, award archives (Site of the Day, Site of the
  Month, Nominees), case studies, and agency directory listings is free without
  requiring an account or subscription.
- **Paid:** Single standard website submissions start at $65 (non-refundable).
  Pro membership subscriptions provide directory visibility and submission
  discounts: Basic at $6.70/month ($80.40/year), Professional at $13.80/month
  ($165.60/year, includes one free annual submission and 30% discount on
  subsequent entries), and International at $324/month billed annually.
  Standalone Academy courses and Creative Pass educational subscriptions are
  sold separately (starting around $12 to $27/month).
- **Sign-up required?** No for browsing inspiration. Yes for submitting
  projects, casting community votes, saving items to personal collections, or
  enrolling in Academy courses.
- **Licence for client work:** Displayed websites remain the intellectual
  property and copyright of their respective creators, agencies, and clients.
  Designs cannot be copied verbatim; they serve as reference benchmarks and
  conceptual inspiration.

Verified on the official Awwwards pricing and submission pages on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Browse and filter categories: Navigate to the Websites archive
   (`https://www.awwwards.com/websites/`) and use the filter dropdowns to narrow
   entries by technology (such as Three.js, GSAP, or Webflow), industry, color,
   or award category (Site of the Day, Developer Award).
2. Deconstruct interaction patterns: Open live winning sites in desktop and
   mobile viewports. Inspect browser DevTools to analyze performance profiles,
   scroll-driven animation timing, canvas implementations, and responsive
   breakpoints.
3. Synthesize design briefs: Capture specific visual treatments—such as
   typography pairings, micro-interactions, or layout grids—and link the live
   examples directly in TolongLabs project RFCs or client mood boards.
4. Benchmark client scope and budget: Use Awwwards references when scoping
   custom client engagements, differentiating standard component-driven layouts
   from custom, award-tier interactive builds.

## When to use it

- Use it when pitching high-concept client builds, luxury brand showcases,
  interactive marketing experiences, or exploratory WebGL/canvas projects.
- Use it when seeking inspiration for innovative layout compositions, fluid
  transitions, and cutting-edge visual trends.
- Skip it when designing utilitarian enterprise dashboards, B2B SaaS data
  tables, or standard CRUD applications; consult
  [Land-book](../design-galleries/land-book.md) or
  [Siteinspire](../design-galleries/siteinspire.md) instead for conversion- and
  usability-focused patterns.

## Agent notes

- Not machine-readable: Awwwards does not provide a public API, npm package, CLI
  tool, or `llms.txt` file for coding agents.
- Agents should treat Awwwards as an external reference URL source when helping
  developers construct creative design briefs or aesthetic direction docs.
- The web app relies on client-rendered media and JavaScript for voting and
  collection management, but public gallery pages are indexable and viewable in
  standard browsers.
- Do not copy site code or proprietary assets directly from showcased sites into
  client codebases due to intellectual property rights.
