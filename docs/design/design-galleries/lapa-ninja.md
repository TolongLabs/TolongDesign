## What it is

Lapa Ninja is an extensive gallery dedicated specifically to responsive landing
page inspiration. Created to help designers, developers, and marketers build
better marketing pages, the site hosts an indexed collection of more than 7,300
individual landing page designs alongside over 15,000 full-page screenshots.

Each entry on Lapa Ninja captures a complete full-page screenshot of the live
product page, allowing visitors to inspect layout rhythms, section sequencing,
pricing presentations, and footer treatments from top to bottom. The archive
indexes desktop views alongside mobile viewports to highlight responsive
adaptations.

Beyond static visual references, Lapa Ninja maintains a dedicated "Freebies"
section featuring downloadable Figma kits, vector illustrations, UI component
kits, and free templates built for platforms like Webflow and Framer.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Browsing all 7,300+ landing page screenshots, filtering by industry
  and style, downloading free UI kits and templates, and submitting websites via
  email are completely free.
- **Paid:** No paid subscription tiers or paid member paywalls.
- **Sign-up required?** No. Browsing designs and downloading featured freebie
  assets require no account registration.
- **Licence for client work:** Screenshots and featured brand designs belong to
  their respective owners and serve as conceptual benchmarks. Downloadable
  freebie templates and UI kits have distinct licenses (often CC0 or MIT);
  inspect individual asset files before shipping.

## How to use

1. Select industry and style tags: Filter by domain (such as SaaS, AI, Dev
   Tools, or Fintech) or visual style (Minimal, 3D, Bento Grid, Retro) to match
   project requirements.
2. Review full-page compositions: Open individual entries to examine how top
   converting websites order their page sections—from hero copy and social proof
   to feature breakdown, pricing tables, and FAQs.
3. Assess mobile responsive shifts: Compare desktop views against responsive
   mobile screenshots to identify how dense tables or multi-column grids fold on
   smaller screens.
4. Synthesize section architecture: Document recommended section sequences and
   component layouts in product design briefs or implementation roadmaps.

## When to use it

- Use it when designing B2B SaaS landing pages, startup launch pages, or
  developer tool product homepages.
- Use it to examine full-page section flow and responsive transitions from hero
  to footer.
- Skip it when seeking high-end interactive WebGL experiments or avant-garde art
  direction; refer to [Awwwards](../design-galleries/awwwards.md) or
  [Hover States](../design-galleries/hover-states.md) instead.

## Agent notes

- Visual gallery without a public REST/GraphQL API, npm package, or `llms.txt`.
- Pages employ Cloudflare bot management, so automated headless scraping will
  trigger challenges.
- Coding agents can use Lapa Ninja category URLs to point developers toward
  concrete landing page patterns and section structure examples.
