## What it is

Early (early.tools) is a curated product discovery directory that spotlights
pre-launch startups, private betas, waitlists, and newly unveiled indie tools.
Founded and operated by Julian Paul (@itsjulianpaul), the platform curates
hundreds of nascent digital software products across developer tools, AI
utilities, productivity apps, and niche workflow systems.

Unlike traditional startup directories focused on established scale-ups or
funding rounds, Early documents products in their earliest phases of public
presentation. This makes it an ideal venue for observing how founders design
high-conversion waitlist landing pages, minimal viable value propositions, and
streamlined onboarding forms.

Visitors can filter through recent product launches, subscribe to weekly roundup
newsletters, and explore emerging product categories to evaluate market
positioning and aesthetic tendencies across early-stage tech.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Browsing the full product catalogue, viewing waitlist links,
  filtering by category, and reading founder resources is free without
  registration. Standard editorial submissions are also evaluated for free.
- **Paid:** Fast-track product submissions and sponsored placement start from
  €19 one-time, which permanently lists the tool and pins it to the top of the
  directory for selected weeks. Dedicated newsletter sponsorships and featured
  placements are priced separately.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is required to browse or search. Product
  submission requires submitting project details through a web form.
- **Licence for client work:** Cataloged products, names, and assets remain the
  property of their respective creators. Insights can be applied freely to
  client waitlist and landing page architectures.

Verified on the official Early submission and sponsor pages on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Visit `https://www.early.tools/` to review newly cataloged early-stage
   projects and waitlist launches.
2. Filter through categories relevant to your TolongLabs project (for example,
   Developer Tools, Design, AI, or Productivity).
3. Analyze how pre-revenue founders articulate complex value propositions with
   minimalist copy, animated product demos, and social proof tags.
4. Borrow proven waitlist mechanics—such as viral referral loops, early-bird
   pricing previews, and access counter cues—for TolongLabs client launches.

- Use it when designing waitlist pages, pre-launch teasers, or MVP landing pages
  for new product initiatives.
