## What it is

Layers is a public design community where members post interface concepts,
product screens, brand work, and prototypes. Explore presents a changing feed,
while tags and text search lead to narrower collections of work.

It is community-submitted work rather than a vetted library of production
interfaces. A listing may be a concept or UI kit, so check the entry and author
before treating it as a shipped product reference.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free** Public Explore and tagged search pages are accessible. Joining is
  free; an account is used for posting and following designers.
- **Paid** No paid membership price or paid access tier was published on the
  official pages checked. Third-party products shown in community posts may be
  sold separately.
- **Sign-up required?** No account is needed to browse public results. A free
  account is needed to participate in the community.
- **Licence for client work** The platform does not grant rights to reuse
  members’ designs, screenshots, or files in a client project. Follow the
  creator’s own license or obtain permission.

Pricing and availability checked 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Open Explore to scan current design work, or search for a concrete topic such
   as pricing plans, dashboards, onboarding, or mobile screens.

2. Use tag pages to narrow broad searches. Compare several authors and
   distinguish concepts from shipped product pages.

3. Open the source project and record its author, design goal, layout pattern,
   and interaction idea. Save the public link rather than the image.

4. Write a brief for the TolongLabs project that states what to borrow at the
   pattern level and what must change for the product’s users, content, and
   brand.

## When to use it

- Use it when you want fresh concepts and community perspectives across web,
  app, and brand work.
- Skip it when you need only live-site references or verified production flows;
  use [Curated Design](../design-galleries/curated-design.md) or
  [Mobbin](../design-galleries/mobbin.md).

## Agent notes

- No official API, MCP server, CLI, or code package was found. Give an agent a

- Treat posted designs as inspiration, not reusable components or licensed
  production assets.
