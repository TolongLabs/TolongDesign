## What it is

posts.design is a curated visual archive founded by digital creator Max Based.
The platform documents what leading technology brands, venture-backed startups,
and creative studios actually ship to their social media channels, indexing real
assets from over 720 modern companies.

In contemporary digital product launches, a website's social presence is as
vital as its landing page. Developers and marketing designers must design
attention-grabbing launch graphics, product release announcements, changelog
cards, and automated OpenGraph social preview images (`og:image`). posts.design
isolates these assets into a searchable, filterable repository.

The platform categorizes assets by functional intent: product launches, feature
explorations, hiring announcements, changelog updates, brand headers, visual
trends, and dedicated OpenGraph preview cards. Each asset includes attribution
to the authoring brand and direct links to the original social posts.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Full access to browse all 720+ brand archives, filter by category
  (launches, OpenGraph, headers, trends), and view high-resolution social assets
  is completely free.
- **Paid:** No subscription fees, pro paywalls, or gated assets.
- **Sign-up required?** No. The entire archive is accessible without an account
  or login.
- **Licence for client work:** Displayed graphics remain the copyright and brand
  assets of their respective companies. They serve as comparative research and
  inspiration for crafting original marketing visuals.

## How to use

1. Select marketing objective: Filter by category (e.g., `/launches` for product
   launch visuals or `/opengraph` for link preview cards).
2. Filter by brand or industry: Study how design-led companies (e.g., Linear,
   Vercel, Supabase, Raycast) craft their visual brand identity on social feeds.
3. Analyze asset composition: Examine typographic balance, dark-mode contrast,
   3D product renders, and UI snippet framing in standard social aspect ratios
   (16:9, 1:1, 1.91:1).
4. Code dynamic OpenGraph templates: Use documented compositions to inform
   automated `@vercel/og` or Satori SVG generation templates in Next.js
   codebases.

## When to use it

- Use it when designing OpenGraph preview images, product launch assets, or
  social campaign graphics for client websites.
- Use it to study how high-growth startups communicate technical features within
  static graphic frames.
- Skip it when designing core in-app UI components or full marketing websites;
  consult [Landingfolio](../design-galleries/landingfolio.md) or
  [SiteInspire](../design-galleries/siteinspire.md) instead.

## Agent notes

- Visual marketing archive without an API or CLI tool.
- Built with Next.js with optimized WebP image delivery.
- Coding agents generating dynamic OpenGraph images (`@vercel/og`, Satori, or
  Canvas) can adapt typographic hierarchies and card layout schemes seen on
  posts.design to construct high-converting social preview templates.
