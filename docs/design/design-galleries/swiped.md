## What it is

Swiped describes itself as a hand-curated swipe file of strong posts from
designers on X and LinkedIn. Its topic areas include brand design, product
design, design engineering and studio business, with weekly updates.

The archive is organized into recent time windows and includes a search and
platform filters. The individual card links were not exposed in the page content
available during this check, so the examples below point to visible archive
sections rather than specific posts.

## How to use

1. Open the current-week, previous-week or previous-month archive section.
2. Filter by X or LinkedIn and search for a topic such as product design or
   studio work.
3. Open the original social post when a card is available to verify its author
   and context.
4. Analyze the post’s structure, evidence and tone rather than copying its
   wording.
5. Use the pattern to write original project updates or design rationale.

**Capture in a TolongLabs design brief:**

- Record the post author and original URL before citing a pattern.
- Note the hook, evidence, explanation and call to action as separate elements.
- Write fresh copy suited to the product audience and channel.

## When to use it

- Use it when studying how designers explain work or write about the design
  industry.
- Skip it when you need stable visual case studies or implementation details.

## Agent notes

No API, CLI, llms.txt or agent connector was visible in the official page
content checked. The site blocks automated browser access, and post cards were
not retrievable at this check.

Before quoting or adapting a post, follow the linked social source and respect
the author’s context and rights.
