## What it is

Lenis describes this page as a collection of “hand-curated templates powered by
Lenis.” The page exposes search and category labels, then links to live
previews, creator sites, and marketplace pages. Lenis does not display a
template checkout, source download, or collection-wide license on this page.

The collection is free to browse, but its linked items have separate access
terms. The sampled Framer Marketplace pages include a free template and paid
templates. The creator or marketplace listing, not Lenis, sets the price and
license for each product.

This is a discovery and reference collection, not a single purchasable Lenis
template source. A preview page is not the production project file. For a
specific template, use its own product listing to confirm what is delivered and
what use is allowed.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** The Lenis directory is readable without a visible paywall. Its page
  also offers an optional email subscription. No Lenis template price or paid
  directory tier was published.
- **Free listing:** Fuel on Framer Marketplace offers “Remix for Free” and shows
  the license label “Limited.” Framer's Community terms give free content a
  limited commercial license, including client work when it is part of a broader
  end product. The terms prohibit sharing or reselling the template as a
  standalone asset.
- **Paid listings:** Rep Republic displays $89 and “Single-Use”; ClearPath
  displays $129 and “Single-Use.” Nakula also displays $129 and “Single-Use.”
  These are sample listing prices, not a Lenis price. Listings show a dollar
  sign but do not state a currency code, billing cadence, taxes, or checkout
  total.
- **Checkout and delivery:** Framer says a paid template checkout takes place on
  the creator's website. The creator sends a remix link, which copies the
  project to a Framer workspace. No checkout was opened.
- **Sign-up required?** The Lenis directory can be browsed without a visible
  account prompt. Framer's documented remix flow copies a project into an
  account or workspace; account sign-in was not tested.
- **Licence for client work:** Framer's terms permit modifying paid content for
  one end product or project and using it commercially within that project. They
  prohibit use in more than one project, website, product, or client engagement,
  and prohibit redistribution or sublicensing. The paid terms do not state
  client handoff rights in the sampled product listings; confirm any source-file
  or account transfer with the creator. Free-content terms explicitly allow
  client work when the asset is part of a broader end product. Non-Framer
  listings have their own terms and need separate review.

Prices and terms checked 2026-10-11. These are third-party listings and may
change. Do not infer template rights from the Lenis core library's license.

## How to use

1. Browse the Lenis page and open a listed preview. Record the exact template
   title, author, destination platform, and preview URL. Treat preview content
   as reference until a specific product license is confirmed.
2. For a Framer template, open its Framer Marketplace listing. Recheck the
   current price, currency, creator checkout, license, refund terms, support,
   and any rights for images, fonts, icons, or other included assets.
3. Before a paid purchase, confirm the approved budget, project and client
   scope, Framer workspace, permission to modify and publish, and whether the
   license covers the intended client relationship. A paid Framer listing is
   limited to one project under the Community terms; do not reuse its source
   project across sites or clients.
4. Framer's documented free flow is to choose “Use for Free,” then copy the
   project into an account. For paid templates, choose “Buy,” complete the
   creator's checkout, use the emailed remix link, and copy the project into a
   workspace. There is no Lenis install command or package to add.
5. Before publishing, replace sample copy and assets and verify forms,
   responsive behavior, motion, reduced-motion behavior, and target-browser
   fallbacks in the copied project. The sampled listings do not document
   reduced-motion or browser-fallback obligations.

## When to use it

- Use it to discover published Framer template previews that Lenis lists as
  powered by Lenis, or to collect URLs for a project brief.
- Use a preview as a reference unless a specific product listing gives rights to
  copy and modify the production project.
- Skip it when a project needs a portable React, Next.js, or other code starter;
  the sampled Framer flow delivers a Framer project to a workspace.
- Skip a paid item until its current price, license scope, and client-use terms
  match the approved project.

## Agent notes

- Official directory: [lenis.dev/templates](https://lenis.dev/templates). The
  captured page text showed search and category controls, preview links, and an
  email subscription prompt. No package, CLI, or source download was exposed.
- Framer Marketplace templates are native Framer projects. Framer's help page
  documents copying them into a Framer account or workspace; it does not
  describe an HTML, React, or Vue source-code export for these listings.
- Framer Community terms distinguish free-content rights from paid single-use
  rights. Check the exact listing and seller terms before client use. The Lenis
  core library's license does not license the template project.
- Keep purchased remix projects, template source, screenshots, and third-party
  images, fonts, and icons out of this reference collection. Store and use any
  licensed source only within the project scope allowed by its terms.
- Rep Republic's listing mentions smooth-scroll animations and a custom cursor.
  The sampled product pages publish no reduced-motion preference, browser
  support matrix, or fallback requirements. Inspect and test the copied project
  before production use.
- Page and product details were read from extracted text. Rendered browser
  inspection was unavailable because the connected browser requested
  reauthentication; no screenshots, sign-ins, forms, or checkouts were used.
