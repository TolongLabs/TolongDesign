## What it is

Cursorful is a screen recording and automated video editing solution built to
democratize cinematic product demo creation. Available as both a lightweight
browser extension (for Chrome, Edge, and Brave) and a dedicated desktop app for
macOS and Windows, Cursorful tracks your cursor and clicks to generate
professional, auto-edited product walkthroughs.

The extension captures the active browser viewport while logging exact DOM click
coordinates. When you finish recording, Cursorful opens an in-browser timeline
editor that automatically places smooth, keyframe-free camera zooms focused on
every interaction. Mouse pointers are redrawn as crisp, customizable vector
cursors with smoothed trajectory paths that eliminate jitter and hesitation.

Because the browser extension operates entirely on client-side WebCodecs and
Canvas APIs, video processing and export happen locally on your device without
uploading sensitive web application interfaces to external servers. It includes
customizable canvas backgrounds, aspect ratio switching (16:9, 9:16, 1:1), and
dynamic webcam shapes.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free (Browser Extension):** $0 forever. Includes unlimited browser
  recordings, automatic zooms, manual zoom timeline adjustments, redrawn
  cursors, aspect ratio toggles, and HD exports with no watermark and no
  mandatory account sign-up.
- **Paid (Cursorful Pro):** $79 one-time payment (limited promotional offer;
  regular list price $119). Includes the native desktop app for macOS and
  Windows, recording outside the browser (native apps, terminals, full screen),
  4K video exports, advanced camera and audio controls, and lifetime updates.
- **Sign-up required?** No for the free extension; required for activating Pro
  desktop license keys.
- **Licence for client work:** Permitted for commercial client presentations,
  marketing videos, and software documentation.

## How to use

1. Install the free Cursorful extension from the Chrome Web Store: Navigate to
   `https://cursorful.com/` and click "Install free".

2. Pin the extension to your browser toolbar.

3. Record your web application:

- Click the Cursorful icon in your browser toolbar.
- Choose your recording mode: current tab or screen area.
- Click "Record" and demonstrate your web application features. Cursorful
  automatically tracks cursor position and clicks.

4. Customize zooms and framing in the editor:

- Stop recording to open the Cursorful in-browser timeline editor.
- Review automatically generated zooms; drag timeline markers to adjust timing
  or modify zoom depth from shallow to deep.
- Choose a gradient background and adjust window corner rounding and shadow.
- Customize the mouse cursor: adjust scale, color, or toggle cursor click
  ripples.

5. Export your video:

- Select 1080p MP4 or GIF format.
- Click **Export** to render the video locally via your browser's GPU.

## When to use it

- Use it when you need to record a quick, polished client demo or bug
  walkthrough directly from your browser without installing heavy desktop
  software.
- Use the free extension as a zero-cost, watermark-free alternative to Loom and
  basic screen recorders.
- Skip it if you need full-system screen recording with terminal capture on a
  100% open-source stack; use [BetterShot](../video-capture/bettershot.md) or
  [Recordly](../video-capture/recordly.md) instead.
- Skip it if you need advanced 3D perspective tilts and realistic scene
  reflections; use [Slant It](../video-capture/slant-it.md) instead.

## Agent notes

- Closed-source browser extension and desktop application.
- All video compilation runs locally inside the browser using WebCodecs.
- Coding agents cannot programmatically trigger recordings via the extension;
  human developers should record demos and export MP4 files into project repos.
