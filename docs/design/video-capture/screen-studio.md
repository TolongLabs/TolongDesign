## What it is

Screen Studio is a premium macOS screen recording and post-production
application created by Adam Pietrasiak. Designed specifically for software
creators, indie hackers, and product teams, it transforms ordinary desktop
recordings into cinematic, highly polished product walkthroughs with virtually
zero manual video editing.

While recording, Screen Studio logs precise mouse cursor movements and click
events alongside the video frames. In post-production, it replaces abrupt mouse
jerks with fluid, bezier-smoothed cursor paths and automatically inserts smooth,
cinematic camera zooms focused directly on the active interactive element.

The application automatically frames the recorded window against customizable
gradient backgrounds, adds realistic drop shadows and rounded window corners,
embeds circular or rectangular selfie webcam cutouts, and generates synchronized
captions. Exports support pristine 4K video at 60fps or high-framerate GIFs.

## How to use

1. Download Screen Studio from `https://screen.studio/` and install on macOS.

2. Grant macOS Screen Recording, Camera, and Accessibility permissions in System
   Settings.

3. Record your web application demo:

- Select the recording area: fullscreen or a specific browser window.
- Enable microphone audio and optionally enable front-facing camera input.
- Click "Record" and perform your user workflow at natural speed. Don't worry
  about mouse hesitation or jitter.

4. Customize automated zoom and cursor effects:

- In the post-capture editor, review automatically placed zoom keyframes.
- Drag zoom anchors along the timeline to adjust timing or click to modify zoom
  depth.
- Adjust cursor settings: customize cursor size, highlight ring color, or click
  sound volume.

5. Style the backdrop and export:

- Choose a gradient or solid background color that complements your client's
  brand palette.
- Adjust window padding, corner radius, and drop shadow intensity.
- Click **Export** and choose 4K MP4 or 60fps GIF for inclusion in README docs
  or landing page hero sections.

## When to use it

- Use it when creating flagship promotional videos, Product Hunt launch media,
  investor pitch demos, or social media teasers where presentation quality is
  paramount.
- Use it to save hours of tedious manual keyframing in After Effects or Premiere
  Pro.
- Skip it if you need an open-source, free screen recorder with automated zooms;
  use [BetterShot](../video-capture/bettershot.md) or
  [Recordly](../video-capture/recordly.md) instead.
- Skip it if you prefer in-browser recording with zero software installs; use
  [Cursorful](../video-capture/cursorful.md) or
  [Tokokino](../video-capture/tokokino.md) instead.
