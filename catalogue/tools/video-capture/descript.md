## What it is

Descript is an AI-first audio and video editing platform that revolutionizes the
editing experience by coupling media playback directly to an interactive text
transcript. Rather than hunting through complex timeline cuts and audio tracks,
users import raw recordings and edit the spoken video exactly as they would edit
a document in Google Docs.

Deleting a sentence or word from the transcript automatically slices that exact
segment from both the video and audio tracks. Descript incorporates its
Underlord AI assistant to automate tedious post-production chores: it detects
and strips filler words ("um", "uh", "you know") in one click, removes
background noise and reverb with Studio Sound, generates viral social media
clips, and creates realistic AI voice clones to patch misspoken dialogue without
re-recording.

For tech companies and design teams, Descript streamlines internal and external
communication. Teams use it to record polished screen walkthroughs, generate
animated captions with custom typography, replace webcam backgrounds with green
screen simulations, and translate audio into dozens of foreign languages with AI
voice dubbing.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** 1 transcription media hour per month, 720p exports with a Descript
  watermark, and 5 Underlord AI credits per month.
- **Paid:**
  - **Hobbyist:** $16 per person/month billed annually ($24 billed monthly). 10
    media hours per month, 1080p watermark-free exports, 10 Underlord
    credits/mo, and voice cloning.
  - **Creator:** $24 per person/month billed annually ($35 billed monthly). 30
    media hours per month, 4K watermark-free exports, 30 Underlord credits/mo,
    unlimited royalty-free stock media, and advanced AI video regeneration.
  - **Business / Enterprise:** $40+ per seat/month for 40+ media hours, custom
    domain branding, SSO, and dedicated team account management.
- **Sign-up required?** Yes, account registration is mandatory.
- **Licence for client work:** Paid plans grant commercial rights to produce and
  distribute client videos, podcasts, and advertising content.

## How to use

1. Download the Descript desktop app for macOS or Windows from
   `https://descript.com/` or open the browser-based editor.

2. Create a new video project and import your recording:

- Record your screen and camera directly inside Descript, or drag in an existing
  MP4 / MOV file.
- Descript automatically transcribes the dialogue with speaker labels in
  seconds.

3. Edit the video via the text transcript:

- Highlight and press `Backspace` on unwanted sections, false starts, or
  tangents; the video timeline cuts automatically.
- Click the "Actions" menu and select "Remove Filler Words" to purge all "ums"
  and "ahs" across the entire timeline simultaneously.

4. Enhance audio and visual elements:

- Toggle **Studio Sound** to transform low-cost microphone audio into
  studio-quality sound with AI noise isolation.
- Click the **Captions** widget to overlay animated dynamic subtitles formatted
  with your client's brand fonts and colors.

5. Export the finished cut:

- Click **Publish** to export a watermark-free 1080p or 4K MP4 file, or generate
  a private web link with an interactive transcript player for client review.

## When to use it

- Use it when producing spoken content: founder updates, technical tutorials,
  customer interviews, or podcasts where cleaning up spoken speech quickly is
  essential.
- Use it to create social video clips with dynamic, word-by-word highlighted
  subtitles.
- Skip it if you need high-framerate code walkthroughs with automatic cursor
  tracking and smooth camera zooms; use
  [Screen Studio](../video-capture/screen-studio.md) instead.
- Skip it if you need programmatic, code-driven video generation in React; use
  [Remotion](../video-capture/remotion.md) instead.

## Agent notes
