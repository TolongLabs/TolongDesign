## What it is

Jessy In's Gallery is a digital art space and collaborative visual moodboard
curated by designer and creative technologist Jessy In (jessyin.world).
Described as a "stroll through the garden of human creativity," the project
presents an eclectic, non-commercial collection of design artefacts, typography
samples, archival photography, and artistic web ephemera.

The project goes beyond static moodboards by incorporating innovative
navigational paradigms. The core gallery (`/`) displays a dynamic masonry wall
of visual discoveries. The "uncovered" mode (`/explore`) presents an interactive
3D globe where each data point corresponds to a curated artwork plotted
according to its creator's geographic origin, visualizing the global breadth of
the collection.

The platform also features a multiplayer mode ("curate together", `/play`),
allowing two concurrent visitors to take turns selecting pieces in real time to
co-create a shared mini-gallery.

## How to use

1. Open `https://gallery.jessyin.world/` to review the visual stream for organic
   color schemes, unexpected textures, and vintage or editorial typography.
2. Navigate to the `uncovered` tab (`/explore`) to rotate the 3D globe and
   discover localized creative movements from different regions worldwide.
3. Use the `curate together` tab (`/play`) for interactive team alignment
   sessions or creative brainstorming with clients and collaborators.
4. Pin specific visual motifs into TolongLabs project mood boards during early-
   stage creative direction sprints.
