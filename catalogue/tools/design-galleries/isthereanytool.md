## What it is

isthereanytool is a searchable directory of design, craft and AI tools. Its home
page groups listings into categories such as visual design, typography, motion
and UI components, and links to editorial “best” guides.

The directory is useful for discovery and comparison, but its summaries are a
starting point. Follow each listing to the vendor’s own documentation for
current capabilities, limits, pricing and licence terms.

## Paywall and pricing

- Free: Public search, categories and editorial guides are available to browse.
- Paid: No reader subscription was published; the site does offer sponsor or
  advertising placements.
- Sign-up required? No for browsing the public directory.
- Licence for client work: Determined by each listed product, not by this
  directory.

The access and pricing details above were checked on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Search by a design task or open a category to scan relevant tools.
2. Use the best-of guides to narrow an initial list, then note the options that
   match your constraints.
3. Open each vendor’s official site and verify pricing, support, data handling
   and licence.
4. Compare only tools that address the same job and fit the project’s stack.
5. Record the selection rationale and any paid-plan or commercial-use limits in
   the brief.

**Capture in a TolongLabs design brief:**

- Name the task, required capabilities and budget before comparing options.
- Capture each candidate’s official pricing and licence link with the check
  date.
- Mark editorial recommendations as discovery leads rather than endorsements.

## When to use it

- Use it when building a shortlist across several kinds of design or creative
  software.
- Skip it when you need verified vendor documentation or an exhaustive market
  audit; check official sources directly.
