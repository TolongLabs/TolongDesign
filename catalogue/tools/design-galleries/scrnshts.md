## What it is

Scrnshts was created in 2019 by art director and product designer Thomas Vimare
as a dedicated curation gallery to help product teams benchmark iOS App Store
and Google Play Store screenshot designs. In 2025, the platform was acquired and
merged into ScreensDesign, with `scrnshts.club` issuing an HTTP 301 redirect to
`screensdesign.com/store-screenshots/`.

The library catalogues thousands of marketing screenshots produced by leading
mobile applications across lifestyle, fintech, productivity, health, and gaming
categories. Rather than showing in-app operational UI alone, it showcases how
top apps frame their value proposition within App Store preview slides.

Product teams use this archive to evaluate visual storytelling conventions: how
teams use panoramic multi-slide graphics, highlight core feature value in bold
headlines, render device bezels, integrate lifestyle photography, and showcase
social proof badges to maximize store conversion rates.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** Browsing featured store screenshots, previewing a selection of top
  apps, and filtering basic categories without payment.
- **Paid:** Full access to the extended ScreensDesign library, video flows, and
  detailed paywall research requires a Pro subscription:
  - Weekly Plan: $29/week (50 credits).
  - Monthly Plan: $29 to $39/month (400 credits).
  - Semiannual Plan: $145/6 months (2,400 credits).
  - Annual Plan: $229 to $299/year (4,800 credits).
- **Sign-up required?** Browsing basic previews is open; downloading high-res
  assets and accessing full historical flow archives requires an account and Pro
  plan.
- **Licence for client work:** Screenshots depict commercial App Store assets
  owned by the respective app publishers. They serve as comparative benchmarks
  and reference inspiration for ASO marketing campaigns.

## How to use

1. Select app category: Filter by sector (e.g., Finance, Health & Fitness, AI,
   or Utilities) to see how competitors position their offerings in app stores.
2. Deconstruct slide sequence storytelling: Analyze the narrative arc across the
   standard 5-to-10 App Store slides—from initial problem hook to feature proof,
   ratings, and final call-to-action.
3. Review graphic treatments: Note headline typography sizes, background
   gradient treatments, 3D device mockup angles, and localized copy adaptations.
4. Draft ASO creative briefs: Reference specific winning screenshot sequences
   when designing marketing assets for client iOS and Android app launches.

## When to use it

- Use it when designing App Store or Google Play Store screenshot preview sets
  for client app launches.
- Use it to study how high-growth apps structure their visual value proposition
  and conversion funnel outside the app itself.
- Skip it when designing web-based SaaS landing pages; refer to
  [Landingfolio](../design-galleries/landingfolio.md) or

## Agent notes

- The original domain `scrnshts.club` permanently redirects to ScreensDesign
  (`https://screensdesign.com/store-screenshots/`).
- The web app runs as a single-page React application with client-side state; it
  does not provide a public REST API or CLI tool.
- Coding agents can advise developers on standard App Store screenshot
  dimensions (e.g., 6.7" and 6.5" displays) and narrative sequencing based on
  category norms.
