## What it is

Rebrand Gallery documents brand identities and redesigns through shots, intros
and bento-style collections. Filters cover shot type, industry, style,
typography and visual feel, helping teams compare a brand system beyond its
logo.

The site also publishes a Pro plan and lists an MCP connection. Its imagery and
brand materials are references from Rebrand or third-party rights holders, not a
library of assets licensed for a new client brand.

## Paywall and pricing

- Free: The free plan includes a limited catalogue and one collection; the
  pricing page described 140+ brands and thousands of shots.
- Paid: Pro offers a 7-day trial and expanded access. The subscription price was
  not legible in the official page text checked on 2026-10-07.
- Sign-up required? No for basic public browsing; collections and Pro features
  may require an account.
- Licence for client work: Site terms say third-party brand content remains with
  its rights holders and restrict redistribution and derivative use.

The access and pricing details above were checked on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Browse Intros, Bentos or Shots depending on whether you need an overview or
   an individual application.
2. Filter by industry, style, shot type, typography or feel to focus the
   reference set.
3. Open a brand page and compare identity applications across the available
   material.
4. Save a collection if your account and plan support that workflow.
5. Write down the design principles you observe and create original
   client-specific work.

**Capture in a TolongLabs design brief:**

- Describe the brand’s positioning and which identity applications matter to the
  project.
- Record design observations such as type, color, image treatment and
  consistency.
- Keep the reference links and rights caveat with the brief; do not reuse or
  redistribute the media.

## When to use it

- Use it when preparing a brand refresh or studying how a visual identity works
  across touchpoints.
- Skip it when you need downloadable client-use assets or permission to
  reproduce a reference.

## Agent notes

The official site lists an MCP connection on its plans. Review the current
Rebrand Pro page and terms before connecting an agent; the setup URL was not
exposed in the pages checked.

The terms restrict scraping, redistribution and some derivative or competing
commercial uses. Agents should use links and descriptive observations, not
ingest or reproduce the gallery media.
