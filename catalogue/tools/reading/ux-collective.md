## What it is

UX Collective is an independent publication founded by Fabricio Teixeira and
Caio Braga. Originally launched on Medium, it has grown into one of the most
widely read user experience publications globally, curating critical essays,
case studies, and strategic perspectives from practicing designers and product
leaders worldwide.

The platform distinguishes itself from tactical UI tutorial blogs by focusing on
high-level thinking: design ethics, the societal impact of software,
organizational dynamics, the evolving role of AI in design workflows, and
critiques of contemporary interface trends. Rather than merely showing how to
build an interface component, UX Collective publishes pieces that examine why
certain design decisions matter and what their consequences are.

In addition to its main publishing platform at `uxdesign.cc` and its dedicated
essays portal at `essays.uxdesign.cc`, the collective curates an annual "State
of UX" trend report (`trends.uxdesign.cc`), an active weekly email newsletter,
and a sister publication called Bootcamp designed for early-career product
designers.

## Paywall and pricing

- **Free:** All articles on `uxdesign.cc` and `essays.uxdesign.cc` are free to
  read without paying. The weekly curated newsletter and annual State of UX
  reports are completely free to subscribe to.
- **Paid:** UX Collective does not charge readers for subscription tiers or
  paywalled content. Authors write independently or through Medium's Partner
  Program, but UX Collective itself offers free public curation.
- **Sign-up required?** No for reading on the web or via RSS. An email address
  is required only if subscribing to the weekly email newsletter.
- **Licence for client work:** Articles and illustrations are copyrighted by
  their respective authors. The concepts and frameworks discussed can be
  referenced and applied freely in client product design and strategy.

Verified on the official UX Collective website and newsletter on 2026-10-07.

## How to use

1. Research strategic UX patterns: Use the essay archive
   (`https://essays.uxdesign.cc/`) to research broader product design
   approaches—such as design system governance, ethical friction in user flows,
   and cognitive load reduction—before architecting a client application.
2. Review annual industry reports: Consult the State of UX archive
   (`https://trends.uxdesign.cc/`) during early discovery phases to identify
   macro trends impacting user expectations and interface aesthetics.
3. Inform design RFCs: Cite published case studies and critical essays when
   justifying user experience tradeoffs, accessibility requirements, or layout
   simplifications to clients or engineering teams.
4. Keep the team informed: Subscribe to the weekly newsletter to track emerging
   industry critiques, new tools, and community discussions.

## When to use it

- Use it when crafting product design strategies, structuring design systems
  philosophy, or exploring human-centered critiques of AI and automation.
- Use it for team reading clubs and professional growth across design and
  engineering disciplines.
- Skip it when looking for immediate, ready-to-copy code snippets or CSS
  animations; reach for [Codrops](../reading/codrops.md) or
  [Smashing Magazine](../reading/smashing-magazine.md) instead.

## Agent notes

- Non-machine-readable: UX Collective does not provide an npm package, CLI tool,
  `llms.txt` file, or public API.
- Cloudflare bot protection may block direct HTTP fetching of `uxdesign.cc`
  pages; use web search or standard browser feeds when retrieving references.
- Content is primarily long-form conceptual prose; agents should cite its
  arguments to help human teams articulate UX rationale in design documents.
