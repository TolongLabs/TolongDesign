## What it is

Abduzeedo is a design publication founded in 2006 and now produced by a
collective of independent writers. Its articles cover branding, typography,
packaging, editorial, digital design, illustration, 3D, photography, and web
work.

The site publishes project features, visual references, and tutorials. It is
useful for broad creative research and for seeing how studios present a finished
design, rather than for production-ready code.

Articles are publicly readable. Visuals, photography, and project work remain
the property of their respective creators; publication access does not grant
reuse rights.

## How to use

- Browse the category navigation or search for a discipline, studio, or visual
  theme.
- Open an article and note the brief, audience, visual system, typography,
  motion, and production choices that relate to the project.
- Collect references with short annotations explaining what to learn from each
  one; avoid copying a distinctive brand treatment.
- Turn the collected observations into a TolongLabs brief with a target
  audience, mood, content needs, and constraints.

## When to use it

- Use it during early visual research when a team needs a wide range of polished
  creative references.
- Skip it for implementation details, accessibility criteria, or measured
  experiment results; use web.dev, Inclusive Components, or Good UI.

## Agent notes

- The site is editorial content, not an asset library. Link references in a
  brief and verify rights before using any image.
- Article features are examples of finished work, not guaranteed documentation
  of the entire design process.
- No public API, MCP endpoint, or open-content license was found in the pages
  reviewed.
- Use the article's categories and tags to narrow research, then record what the
  reference contributes to the project.
