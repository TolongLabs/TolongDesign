# App launch

> Show a phone app that turns a backlog of video ideas into finished, shareable
> renders.

## Story

The one-line test: creators sit on a someday list of videos (problem); the app
holds that list on their phone and renders each idea (product); it is a registry
of scenes, templates and renders you can change and share (proof); so build
launch videos anywhere and pass them on with a link (ask). The film opens dark
on the app icon, spends its middle on paper listing ideas, dives through a giant
word into the phone, tours the product surfaces, and closes on a quiet lockup.
Light carries the story; dark marks the icon, the dive and nothing else.

## Rhythm

Dark opens the film for 104 frames, then a hard cut flips to paper for beats 2
and 3. A second hard cut flips back to ink for the dive at frame 421, and a
third returns to paper at frame 497 for the rest of the film. Only these three
cuts are hard; every other beat joins continuously or on a whip. Energy peaks
where the film moves fastest: the icon burst at the end of beat 1, the list
scroll in beat 2, the word dive in beat 4, and the caption slides in beat 8.
Long holds in beats 3, 8, 9 and 11 keep each line readable before the film moves
on.

## Rebuild the whole film

1. Cut the three act flips first: dark icon, paper list and hook, dark dive,
   paper product tour.
2. Build the icon ignition: tiles, rose light, dot burst, hold.
3. Type and scroll the idea list, then whip into the two-line hook.
4. Type the small line and dive the camera through the outline word.
5. Fold the screen wall down to the hero phone.
6. Stage the registry line, the three icon rows, and the captioned phone.
7. Build the share line, roll the library phone away, and assemble the lockup.
