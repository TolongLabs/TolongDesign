# Why switch

> Argue against keyframe tools and for a component library that renders
> timelines from code.

## Story

The one-line test: old tools promise a video in a day but bury you in keyframes
(problem); snapcn does anything a timeline does, from scratch or from your repo
(product); your source stays private, the video ships as a live link, and the
component lands in your repo in seconds (proof); so take the components (ask).
The film runs dark for the complaint, flips to paper for the product, and
alternates acts as it stacks capability, privacy, sharing and install before a
still lockup.

## Rhythm

The film opens dark for two beats, flips to paper with a hard cut at frame 184,
returns to ink at frame 315, and flips back to paper at frame 595. The privacy
beat breaks the pattern: it starts on paper and hard-cuts to black inside the
beat at frame 793, then flips to paper again at frame 812. All other beats join
continuously. Energy peaks at the keyframe pile-up, the glow intro, and the mask
click. Long holds — the sources card, the live link, the whole install beat —
keep every claim readable.
