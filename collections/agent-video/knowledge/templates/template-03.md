# Feature drop

> One requested feature becomes a rendered video: ask, build, preview, launch.

## Story

A user asks for one feature and the product delivers it. The problem is cutting
teasers by hand. The product takes a recording, builds three cuts, and spins up
the scenes. The proof is the tablet preview and the finished line. The ask is to
launch today.

The film runs 19.73 s over 9 beats. It opens dark, stays dark, and closes on a
question. One hard cut lands at frame 116. The energy peaks in the reply and
build beats.
