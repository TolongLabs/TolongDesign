# What's new

> A release-notes film: tease the update, name what shipped, prove the fixes,
> tour the app, and publish the launch video.

## Story

Problem: users missed what changed. Product: the new release with scenes,
renders, and captions. Proof: the fixed reports, the app tour, and the shipped
checklist. Ask: publish the launch film and open the link.

The film hooks on giant type, reframes fixes as features, dives through a search
bar into the headline, states the promise in one line, stacks the reported bugs,
tours the phone app, checks off the shipped list, renders the film, previews and
publishes it, and closes on the domain.

## Rhythm

Dark and light swap on every hard cut except the step-log-to-preview join, which
match-cuts inside the light act. The hook stutters with flicker cuts at 59 and
63, then stutters again at 177, 181, and 185 into the dive. Measured cuts fall
at 59, 63, 94, 177, 181, 185, 189, 298, 358, 515, 683, 764, 922, and 999, with
an internal cut at 909 when the preview pane opens. Energy peaks at the
glow-swap exit (43.46, the film high) and the outline dive (21.37); the mid
beats run calm, with the promise line near still. The 168-frame phone tour is
the longest beat; the checklist and promise beats hold longest per frame.

## Rebuild the whole film

1. Cut the flickering outline hook and whip into the glowing swap line.
2. Dive through the search bar into the headline and stagger the icon tiles.
3. State the one-line promise, then stack the reported bugs as chips.
4. Tour the four phone screens in one lateral travel.
5. Check off the shipped list in stagger.
6. Stream the agent step log to the rendered result.
7. Preview the film in the desktop window, open the share modal, and press
   Publish.
8. Assemble the lockup and hold on the domain.
