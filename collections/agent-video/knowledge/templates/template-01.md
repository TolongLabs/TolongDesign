# Product launch

> A bright launch film that moves from a pile of work to agents that ship, for a
> product announcement.

## Story

Problem: the team ships but has no launch video. Product: a component kit that
builds launch films. Proof: screens, tables, and review cards working on screen.
Ask: none on screen except the closing lockup; the film sells the workflow
itself. The film opens on the era headline, piles up the work as pills and
screens, draws the outcome curve, shows the data table and the control cards,
lands the promise line, and closes on the mark.

## Rhythm

The film stays on light for all 10 beats, so there are no act flips. One hard
cut fires at frame 90 into the device wall; every other joint is a match cut
that carries scale or position across the boundary. Holds arrive late: the
control cards hold twice mid-beat, the promise line holds its underline beats,
and the lockup holds its last 30 frames. Energy peaks early in the pill marquee,
then falls: the opening beats run hot, the middle table and card beats run
steady, and the last two beats sit nearly still.

## Rebuild the whole film

1. Cut the paper stage and lock the light palette first.
2. Build beats 1–3 as one continuous push: headline, shrinking pill, scrolling
   pills.
3. Hard-cut to the device wall, then drift through the collage caption.
4. Draw the arrow beat and hold the outcome line.
5. Rise the data table, pan to the agent panel, track the control cards.
6. Build the promise line with two underline sweeps.
7. Reveal the lockup on the grid and hold to the end.
