# Startup pitch

> A demo-day pitch on warm paper: problem, product, numbers, ask, lockup.

## Story

Founders still pitch with static slides while investors want proof. The product
turns one deck into live evidence: a dashboard, a revenue chart ahead of plan,
and an action the AI suggests. The proof is the $1.2M gap in Q4. The ask is to
turn every meeting into a yes.

The film runs 19.43 s over 10 beats. Every beat stays on warm paper. No hard
cuts ship with the template; beats join on the shared ground. Energy peaks in
the opening drift and the product tilt, then settles into held type and the
lockup.
