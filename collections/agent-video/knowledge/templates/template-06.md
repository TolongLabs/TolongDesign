# Launch film

> Three words stake the claim, every scene renders at once, one tap ships it,
> then the offer, the proof, and the mark.

## Story

Making launch videos the slow way hurts; snapcn renders every scene in parallel
and ships the film in one tap, with the paywall and a benchmark to prove it, so
launch it today. Dark acts carry the claim, the tap, and the proof; light acts
carry the work, the offer, and the mark. The film runs 797 frames (26.57 s)
across 7 beats, with hard cuts on every beat boundary except the press-to-burst
handoff.

## Rhythm

Acts alternate hard: dark, light, dark, then the burst flips dark to light
mid-beat, then light, dark, light. Hard cuts land on every beat boundary except
the press-to-burst handoff at frame 363, which plays continuous — the filled
chip becomes the burst's seed. Holds sit inside the work beats (fanout rows,
paywall states) and the whole logo beat is one 110-frame hold. Energy climbs
through the hook (7.78), stays low through the fanout middle, runs steady
through the chip rail, detonates in the burst (10.05, the film's peak), then
drains: the paywall barely moves and the benchmark and logo settle into
stillness.

## Rebuild the whole film

1. Open dark with the three-word hook over grid, ring, and particles, cutting
   hard between words.
2. Cut to paper for the parallel-render step log.
3. Cut to black for the chip rail, land on the render chip, and flow without a
   cut into the coral burst.
4. Cut to the phone paywall on paper and play the convert-and-return arc.
5. Cut to black for the benchmark bars, then to paper for the type-and-delete
   lockup.
6. Keep the single coral accent for the chip fill, the burst, the prices, and
   the winning bar.
